2026 Modernization: Pappus, Pascal’s Hexagrammum Mysticum & Interactive Dossiers¶

Completed a ground-up modernization of geometor/pappus (v0.1.0) to 2026 GEOMETOR ecosystem standards:

  • Namespace Package Architecture: Built src/geometor/pappus/ (projective, constructions, pascal, render, __main__) with PEP 621 pyproject.toml, Python >=3.13 typing, and Google-style docstrings verified cleanly under ruff.

  • Revived & Extended Projective & Conic Discoveries:

    • Universal homogeneous polynomial proofs for both Pappus’s two-line Hexagon Theorem (verify_pappus_symbolic() == 0 over $\mathbb{Z}[a_0, a_1, a_2, b_0, b_1, b_2, m, c]$) and Blaise Pascal’s 1640 smooth-conic Hexagrammum Mysticum (verify_pascal_symbolic() == 0 over $\mathbb{Z}[t_0, t_1, t_2, t_3, t_4, t_5]$).

    • Unified smooth conics and degenerate Pappus line-pairs via exact $6 \times 6$ Veronese rank (are_coconic) and added Michel Chasles’s observer-independent conic cross-ratio (conic_cross_ratio).

    • Enumerated and verified in exact homogeneous coordinates the complete 60-line *Hexagrammum Mysticum* web on the Pythagorean unit circle: 15 chords, 45 diagonal points, 60 Pascal lines, 20 Jakob Steiner points, 60 Thomas Kirkman points, and 15 Julius Plücker lines, showing directly how Pappus’s 2 Star points $(S_+, S_-)$ are the degenerate limit of one bi-triangle Steiner family $(G_+, G_-)$.

    • Revived the homologous square / circle / diamond type triangles from pappus-3.py and the 2023 parallel configuration from pappus.py ($S_+ = (90/73, 38/73)$, $S_- = (10, 38/3)$), plus 19 base / 81 full exact collinear harmonic tetrads in preset="harmonic".

    • Uncovered with geometor.divine two emergent Golden Ratio sections over $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$: / A1 M12 B2 / on Pappus’s golden cross-join and / M25 P4 P0 / at $M_{25} = (-\phi^{-1}, -\phi)$ on Pascal’s golden conic!

  • Interactive SVG Gallery, Test Suite & Theorem Dossiers: Generated 18 dark/light-aware svg.euclid-graphic diagrams, verified 59 pytest cases, and authored five synchronized :g: / .. step:: Sphinx theorem dossiers.

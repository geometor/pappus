Synagoge Multi-Track Book Codex & Books III–VIII Symbolic Constructions (v0.2.0)¶

Expanded geometor/pappus (v0.2.0) into a unified Digital Scriptorium edition and exact symbolic geometry package housing both Pappus of Alexandria’s Synagoge (Μαθηματικὴ Συναγωγή, c. 340 CE) and executable SymPy / geometor.model demonstrations:

  • Five-Unit Four-Track Book Codex (docsrc/synagoge/, :layout: book):

    • Inspected 300 DPI scans of Friedrich Hultsch’s 3-volume critical Greek edition (Weidmann, 1876–1878) across Books III, IV, V, VII, and VIII, pairing diplomatic polytonic Greek (-el) with 100% original GEOMETOR English translations (-en), algebraic summaries (-summary), and philological commentary (-notes) referencing Federico Commandino (1588) and Sir Thomas Little Heath (1921/1926).

    • Built structured JSON apparatus in resources/synagoge/ (sources_manifest.json, citations.json, persons.json, glossary.json, concordance.json) and matching Sphinx apparatus pages, including bidirectional resolution of all 8 pappus:collectio* keys in geometor/euclid.

    • Added geometor.pappus.sphinx_ext registering the -el Greek companion track in photon_platform.publish.book and offline sibling objects.inv intersphinx mappings.

  • Exact Symbolic *Synagoge* Constructions (geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos):

    • Book III (Props. 28–30): construct_three_means exhibiting $A, G, H$ in one semicircle ($A \cdot H = G^2$) with exact orthogonal foot $F$ on $OB$ and external harmonic tangent conjugate $(A, C; D, T) = -1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$.

    • Book IV (Prop. 1): construct_pappus_area_theorem and 10-variable universal polynomial identity verify_pappus_area_symbolic() == 0 generalizing Elements I.47 to arbitrary parallelograms on any scalene triangle.

    • Book IV (Props. 14–18): construct_pappus_arbelos_chain and verify_arbelos_chain_symbolic proving $y_n = n\,d_n = 2n\,r_n$ and focal ellipse locus incidence in 100% rational coordinates.

    • Book V (Prop. 1) & Book VIII (Prop. 23): verify_honeycomb_isoperimetry and construct_honeycomb_isoperimetry proving $n \in \{3, 4, 6\}$ planar tiling uniqueness, $A_6 : A_4 : A_3 = 3\sqrt{3} : 4 : 3$, and the 7-hexagon circle circumradius $R = s_6\sqrt{7}$.

  • Interactive SVG Gallery, CLI & Demos: Extended render.py with exact circular arc support (_extra_arcs), added pappus synagoge and pappus build --synagoge CLI commands generating 24 .euclid-graphic SVGs, added 4 new standalone scripts in demos/ (9 total), and authored docsrc/theorems/synagoge-constructions/index.rst plus tests/test_synagoge.py.

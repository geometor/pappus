Changelog¶

0.2.2 (2026-11-14)¶

  • Standardized top-level About page (docsrc/about/index.rst promoted to top header :order: 10) with the three-pillar GEOMETOR semantic-extraction mission and archival provenance; retired docsrc/mission/ stubs cleanly into Home and About.

0.2.1 (2026-11-14)¶

  • Standardized Book Title Page, top-level header navigation (Book, People, Bibliography, Usage), Reader’s Guide (docsrc/usage/), featured Author and Translator profiles in People, and English structural terminology.

0.2.0 (2026-11-12)¶

  • Multi-Track *Synagoge* Book Codex (docsrc/synagoge/): Added a 5-unit, 4-track (-en, -el, -summary, -notes) Book Codex (:layout: book) covering Books III, IV, V, VII, and VIII of Pappus of Alexandria’s Synagoge (Μαθηματικὴ Συναγωγή, c. 340 CE), pairing Friedrich Hultsch’s 1876–1878 critical polytonic Greek text with 100% original GEOMETOR English translations, bidirectional geometor/euclid citation concordance, Index Nominum, bibliography, and Greek/Latin mathematical glossary.

  • Exact Symbolic *Synagoge* Demonstrations (geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos):

    • construct_three_means (Book III, Props. 28–30): arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic means inside a single semicircle ($A cdot H = G^2$) with exact orthogonal foot projection $F$ and external harmonic tangent conjugate $(A, C; D, T) = -1$ over $mathbb{Q}$ and $mathbb{Q}(phi)$.

    • construct_pappus_area_theorem & verify_pappus_area_symbolic (Book IV, Prop. 1): Pappus’s equiaffine parallelogram generalization of Elements I.47 plus a universal 10-variable polynomial proof over $mathbb{Z}$.

    • construct_pappus_arbelos_chain & verify_arbelos_chain_symbolic (Book IV, Props. 14–18): infinite tangent circle chain inside the arbelos in 100% rational coordinates proving $y_n = n,d_n = 2n,r_n$ and focal ellipse locus invariance.

    • verify_honeycomb_isoperimetry & construct_honeycomb_isoperimetry (Book V, Prop. 1 & Book VIII, Prop. 23): Diophantine proof of unique regular planar tessellations $n in {3, 4, 6}$, exact $mathbb{Q}(sqrt{3})$ area ratios, and 7-hexagon circle inscription ($R = s_6sqrt{7}$).

  • Sphinx Extension, CLI & Gallery Expansion: Added geometor.pappus.sphinx_ext (registering the -el Greek companion track and offline sibling objects.inv intersphinx), pappus synagoge and pappus build --synagoge CLI commands, 6 new .euclid-graphic SVGs (24 total), 4 new standalone demos/ scripts (9 total), and comprehensive tests/test_synagoge.py verification.

0.1.0 (2026-11-11)¶

  • Modernized repository to PEP 621 pyproject.toml (name = "geometor-pappus", Python >=3.13, ruff type/docstring enforcement) and src/geometor/pappus/ namespace layout.

  • Added geometor.pappus.projective with exact SymPy cross-ratios, Book VII Prop. 129 central perspectivities, Wildberger quadrances and spreads, algebraic fourth harmonic conjugates, Veronese \(6 \times 6\) coconicity (are_coconic, conic_equation_through_points), Chasles observer-independent conic cross-ratios (conic_cross_ratio), and collinear harmonic/golden range scanners integrated with geometor.divine.

  • Added geometor.pappus.constructions implementing construct_pappus_hexagon (Synagoge VII.138 & VII.139, ideal cross-join handling, homologous square/circle/diamond triangles), construct_pappus_configuration (all 6 \(S_3\) permutations and Steiner Star concurrency points \(S_+, S_-\)), construct_harmonic_fourth (straightedge complete quadrilateral), and verify_pappus_symbolic.

  • Added geometor.pappus.pascal implementing Blaise Pascal’s Hexagrammum Mysticum (1640) across circles and ellipses (construct_pascal_hexagon), bi-triangle \(S_3\) Steiner concurrency families (construct_pascal_steiner_family), complete homogeneous enumeration of all 15 chords, 45 diagonal points, 60 Pascal lines, 20 Steiner points, 60 Kirkman points, and 15 Plücker lines (enumerate_hexagrammum_mysticum), universal symbolic proof verify_pascal_symbolic, and emergent golden conic section / M25 P4 P0 /.

  • Added geometor.pappus.render and pappus CLI (build, inspect, harmonics, pascal) producing 18 dark/light-aware interactive svg.euclid-graphic figures (including smooth <circle> and <ellipse> conics).

  • Added five standalone runnable demonstrations in demos/ and a 59-test pytest verification suite in tests/test_pappus.py.

  • Authored interactive Sphinx documentation under docsrc/ with step-by-step :g: walkthroughs and five theorem dossiers.

0.0.1 (2022-12-22)¶

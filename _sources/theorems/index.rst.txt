Theorems
========

Structured symbolic explorations tracing Pappus's fourth-century Alexandrian *Synagoge* across
both its projective incidence canon (cross-ratios, complete-quadrilateral harmonic conjugates,
Pappus's Hexagon, Jakob Steiner's six-line configuration, and Golden Ratio perspectivities)
and its classical Books III, IV, V, and VIII constructions (semicircle means, parallelogram areas,
*arbelos* circle chains, and honeycomb isoperimetry).

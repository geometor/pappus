References
==========

.. note::

   **Editorial & Translation Provenance**: Every Greek ``-el`` track in the :doc:`Synagoge Multi-Track Book Codex <../synagoge/index>` follows **Friedrich Hultsch's** public-domain critical Greek edition (*1876–1878*, established from *Codex Vaticanus Graecus* 218), and **100% of the English ``-en`` prose** is translated freshly from Hultsch's Greek by **GEOMETOR** (cross-checked against **Commandino's** 1588 Latin *editio princeps* and **Heath's** 1921 mathematical analysis). Modern scholarly editions and studies listed below are cited strictly as secondary bibliographic references.

Primary Public-Domain Editions & Classical Sources
--------------------------------------------------

- **Hultsch, Friedrich** (ed., 1876–1878). *Pappi Alexandrini Collectionis quae supersunt e libris manu scriptis edidit Latina interpretatione et commentariis instruxit Fridericus Hultsch* (3 vols.). Berlin: Weidmann. *(Vol. I, 1876: Books II–V; Vol. II, 1877: Books VI–VII; Vol. III, 1878: Book VIII & Index Graecitatis. Authoritative public-domain Greek witness for this Codex.)*
- **Commandino, Federico** (trans., 1588). *Pappi Alexandrini Mathematicae Collectiones a Federico Commandino Urbinate in Latinum conversae, et commentariis illustratae*. Pisauri (Pesaro): Hieronymus Concordia. *(The Renaissance Latin editio princeps through which Stevin, Guldin, Descartes, Desargues, Fermat, Pascal, and Newton encountered Pappus.)*
- **Heath, Sir Thomas Little** (1921). *A History of Greek Mathematics, Vol. II: From Aristarchus to Diophantus*. Oxford: Clarendon Press (Chapter XIX: *Pappus of Alexandria*, pp. 355–439). *(Public-domain mathematical commentary across Books II–VIII.)*
- **Pascal, Blaise** (1640). *Essay pour les coniques*. Paris (broadsheet, February 1640). *(Announces the Mystic Hexagram lemma generalizing Pappus's two-line theorem VII.138–139 to any smooth conic section.)*

Modern Secondary Scholarship on Pappus
--------------------------------------

- **Jones, Alexander** (1986). *Pappus of Alexandria: Book 7 of the Collection* (2 vols., Sources in the History of Mathematics and Physical Sciences 8). New York: Springer-Verlag. *(Modern critical study of Book VII and the lost treatises of the Treasury of Analysis.)*
- **Cuomo, Serafina** (2000). *Pappus of Alexandria and the Mathematics of Late Antiquity*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. *(Contextual analysis of Book III's address to Pandrosion, Book V's honeycomb preface, and Book VIII's Alexandrian mechanics.)*
- **Sefrin-Weis, Heike** (2010). *Pappus of Alexandria: Book 4 of the Collection*. London: Springer. *(Historical study of Book IV's Area Theorem, arbelos circle chain, and plane/solid/linear problem classification.)*

Modern Projective, Algebraic & Constructive Geometry
----------------------------------------------------

- **Steiner, Jakob** (1828 / 1832). "Questions proposées: Théorèmes sur l'hexagramme de Pascal," *Annales de mathématiques pures et appliquées* 18 (1828), 339–340; and *Systematische Entwickelung der Abhängigkeit geometrischer Gestalten von einander*, Berlin: Fincke, 1832. *(Discovers the 20 Steiner concurrency points of the 60 Pascal lines and the parity concurrency of the six* :math:`S_3` *Pappus lines.)*
- **Plücker, Julius** (1830). "Über ein neues Princip der Geometrie und den Gebrauch allgemeiner Symbole und unbestimmter Coefficienten," *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik* 5, 268–286. *(Proves that Steiner's 20 points lie four-by-four on 15 Plücker lines rather than 5.)*
- **Kirkman, Thomas Penyngton** (1850). "Note on an Unanswered Prize Question," *Cambridge and Dublin Mathematical Journal* 5, 255–262. *(Proves the second 3-fold concurrency of the 60 Pascal lines at 60 Kirkman points.)*
- **Chasles, Michel** (1865). *Traité des sections coniques*. Paris: Gauthier-Villars. *(Establishes the observer-independent cross-ratio of four points on an irreducible conic.)*
- **Salmon, George** (1879). *A Treatise on Conic Sections* (6th ed.). London: Longmans, Green, and Co. *(Note on Pascal's Mystic Hexagram cataloging the complete 60-line Steiner–Plücker–Kirkman web.)*
- **Hilbert, David** (1899). *Grundlagen der Geometrie*. Leipzig: Teubner. *(Chapter VI proves that Pappus's Hexagon Theorem in a projective plane is logically equivalent to the commutativity of multiplication in its coordinate division ring; see ``geometor/hilbert``.)*
- **Coxeter, H. S. M.** (1987). *Projective Geometry* (2nd ed.). New York: Springer-Verlag. *(Comprehensive synthetic treatment of perspectivities, projectivities, harmonic conjugates, Pappus's configuration* :math:`9_3`, *Pascal's conic hexagram, and Steiner's theorem.)*
- **Hales, Thomas C.** (2001). "The Honeycomb Conjecture," *Discrete & Computational Geometry* 25, 1–22. *(Proves Pappus's Book V honeycomb optimality over arbitrary curved planar partitions.)*
- **Richter-Gebert, Jürgen** (2011). *Perspectives on Projective Geometry: A Guided Tour Through Real and Complex Geometry*. Berlin: Springer. *(Homogeneous cross-product bracket algebra and automated theorem proving for incidence configurations.)*
- **Wildberger, Norman J.** (2005). *Divine Proportions: Rational Trigonometry to Universal Geometry*. Sydney: Wild Egg. *(Defines algebraic quadrance* :math:`Q(A,B)` *and line spread* :math:`s(l_1,l_2)` *over arbitrary fields without transcendental functions; see ``geometor/wildberger``.)*

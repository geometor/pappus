Carpus
======

**Carpus of Antioch** (*Κάρπος ὁ Ἀντιοχεύς*) — 1st/2nd cent. CE
  Mechanician ("Carpus the Engineer") praised alongside Archimedes, Philo, and Hero in Pappus's Book VIII Preface.
  *Hultsch loci*: III p. 1026.

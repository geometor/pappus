Apollonius
==========

**Apollonius of Perga** (*Ἀπολλώνιος ὁ Περγαῖος*) — c. 240–190 BCE
  The "Great Geometer"; central author of Pappus's *Treasury of Analysis* (20 of the 33 books) as well as Book II's myriad-tetrad arithmetic.
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 2–29; II pp. 636–1004.

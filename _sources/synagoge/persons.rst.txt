.. _codex-persons:

Index Nominum (Persons)
=======================

Prosopographical register of all **26** ancient geometers, fourth-century Alexandrian dedicatees, mechanicians, commentators, and modern geometers referenced across the *Synagoge* Codex. Every ``:person:`` reference resolves directly to its anchor below.

Pappus & His Fourth-Century Alexandrian Circle
----------------------------------------------

.. _person-pappus:

**Pappus of Alexandria** (*Πάππος ὁ Ἀλεξανδρεύς*) — fl. c. 320–340 CE
  Last great synthetic geometer of Greek antiquity; compiled the eight-book *Synagoge* preserving and extending the *Treasury of Analysis*, projective cross-ratio and hexagon theorems, arbelos circle chains, honeycomb isoperimetry, and Alexandrian mechanics.
  *Hultsch loci*: I–III passim (pp. 2–1115).

.. _person-pandrosion:

**Pandrosion of Alexandria** (*Πανδροσίων*) — fl. c. 330 CE
  Alexandrian woman mathematician and teacher of geometry, dedicatee of *Synagoge* Book III (*ὦ κρατίστη Πανδροσίων*, restored by Hultsch from feminine vocative manuscript readings where Commandino had assumed masculine).
  *Hultsch loci*: I p. 30, 3; pp. 30–175.

.. _person-megethius:

**Megethius** (*Μεγέθιος*) — fl. c. 335 CE
  Dedicatee of *Synagoge* Book V (*On Isoperimetric Figures*), addressed in the celebrated Preface on the geometrical forethought of bees.
  *Hultsch loci*: I p. 304, 2.

.. _person-hermodorus:

**Hermodorus** (*Ἑρμόδωρος*) — fl. c. 340 CE
  Son of Pappus (*ὦ τέκνον Ἑρμόδωρε*) and dedicatee of both *Synagoge* Book VII (*Treasury of Analysis*) and Book VIII (*Mechanics*).
  *Hultsch loci*: II p. 634, 3; III p. 1022, 3.

.. _person-hierius:

**Hierius** (*Ἱέριος*) — fl. c. 330 CE
  Philosopher and friend of Pappus who proposed mean-proportional constructions examined in Book III.
  *Hultsch loci*: I p. 68.

Classical, Hellenistic & Imperial Greek Authorities
---------------------------------------------------

.. _person-dinostratus:

**Dinostratus** (*Δεινόστρατος*) — fl. c. 350 BCE
  Brother of Menaechmus; applied Hippias's quadratrix (*τετραγωνίζουσα*) to the rectification and quadrature of the circle, analyzed critically (with Sporus's objection) in *Synagoge* Book IV.
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 250–258.

.. _person-aristaeus:

**Aristaeus the Elder** (*Ἀρισταῖος ὁ πρεσβύτερος*) — fl. c. 320 BCE
  Elder contemporary of Euclid who authored *Five Books of Solid Loci* (conic sections) in the *Treasury of Analysis* and compared the five regular polyhedra.
  *Hultsch loci*: I p. 132; II pp. 636, 672–678.

.. _person-euclid:

**Euclid** (*Εὐκλείδης*) — fl. c. 300 BCE
  Praised by Pappus in Book VII for gentle modesty toward predecessors; author of *Elements* (generalized in `IV.1`), *Data*, the lost three-book *Porisms* (source of `VII.129–139`), and the lost two-book *Surface Loci* (source of `VII.238` focus–directrix).
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 72, 176–179; II pp. 636–660, 676, 866–918, 1004–1014.

.. _person-archimedes:

**Archimedes of Syracuse** (*Ἀρχιμήδης ὁ Συρακούσιος*) — c. 287–212 BCE
  Revered throughout Books IV, V, and VIII: source of the arbelos, spiral quadrature, the 13 semi-regular solids preserved solely in Book V (`V.19`), center-of-gravity mechanics, and the famous saying *«δός μοι ποῦ στῶ καὶ κινῶ τὴν γῆν»* (`VIII`, p. 1060).
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 208–245, 312, 352–358; III pp. 1024, 1060.

.. _person-eratosthenes:

**Eratosthenes of Cyrene** (*Ἐρατοσθένης ὁ Κυρηναῖος*) — c. 276–194 BCE
  Invented the mesolabe for two mean proportionals (Book III pp. 56–58) and composed two lost books *On Means* (*Περὶ μεσοτήτων*) closing Pappus's 33-book *Treasury of Analysis* (Book VII p. 636).
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 56–58; II pp. 636, 662, 672.

.. _person-nicomedes:

**Nicomedes** (*Νικομήδης*) — 3rd cent. BCE
  Introduced the conchoidal curves (*κογχοειδεῖς γραμμαί*) presented in *Synagoge* Books III and IV for solving solid neusis problems, duplicating the cube, and trisecting rectilinear angles.
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 56–62, 242–250.

.. _person-apollonius:

**Apollonius of Perga** (*Ἀπολλώνιος ὁ Περγαῖος*) — c. 240–190 BCE
  The "Great Geometer"; central author of Pappus's *Treasury of Analysis* (20 of the 33 books) as well as Book II's myriad-tetrad arithmetic.
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 2–29; II pp. 636–1004.

.. _person-zenodorus:

**Zenodorus** (*Ζηνόδωρος*) — 2nd cent. BCE
  Author of *On Isoperimetric Figures*; proved that among isoperimetric $n$-gons the regular one is greatest and the circle exceeds every regular polygon—recast and extended by Pappus in Book V.
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 308–350.

.. _person-heron:

**Hero of Alexandria** (*Ἥρων ὁ Ἀλεξανδρεύς*) — 1st cent. CE
  Chief authority of Pappus's Book VIII on theoretical and practical mechanics, centers of gravity, the five simple machines, toothed gear-trains, the endless screw, and the *Baroulkos* weight-hauler.
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 62–64; III pp. 1022–1073, 1104–1115.

.. _person-nicomachus:

**Nicomachus of Gerasa** (*Νικόμαχος ὁ Γερασηνός*) — c. 60–120 CE
  Neo-Pythagorean arithmetician who classified ten numerical medieties in his *Introduction to Arithmetic*, cited by Pappus in Book III (pp. 68–70).
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 68–85.

.. _person-carpus:

**Carpus of Antioch** (*Κάρπος ὁ Ἀντιοχεύς*) — 1st/2nd cent. CE
  Mechanician ("Carpus the Engineer") praised alongside Archimedes, Philo, and Hero in Pappus's Book VIII Preface.
  *Hultsch loci*: III p. 1026.

.. _person-proclus:

**Proclus Lycius Diadochus** (*Πρόκλος ὁ Διάδοχος*) — 412–485 CE
  Neoplatonic scholarch and author of *Commentary on Euclid Book I* (``geometor/proclus``); preserves Pappus's symmetry proof of *Elements* `I.5` and complements *Synagoge* VII on analysis and porisms.
  *Hultsch loci*: cf. Friedlein 189, 197, 249–250, 429.

Renaissance & Modern Editors and Geometers
------------------------------------------

.. _person-commandino:

**Federico Commandino** — 1509–1575
  Translated Books III–VIII of Pappus's *Synagoge* into Latin with geometric diagrams and commentary (published posthumously at Pesaro in 1588), igniting the seventeenth-century rebirth of analytic and projective geometry.
  *Edition*: Pesaro 1588.

.. _person-guldin:

**Paul Guldin, S.J.** — 1577–1643
  Published the volume-of-revolution centroid rule in his *Centrobaryca* (1635–1641), giving rise to the joint name **Pappus–Guldinus Theorem** (*Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II p. 682).
  *Locus*: cf. II p. 682.

.. _person-descartes:

**René Descartes** — 1596–1650
  Made Pappus's Three-, Four-, and $n$-Line Locus problem (*Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II pp. 676–680) the centerpiece of his 1637 *La Géométrie*.
  *Locus*: cf. II pp. 676–680.

.. _person-desargues:

**Girard Desargues** — 1591–1661
  Revived Pappus's Book VII projective lemmas on cross-ratio, harmonic division, and involution in his 1639 *Brouillon project*, inspiring young Blaise Pascal.
  *Locus*: cf. II pp. 870–918.

.. _person-pascal:

**Blaise Pascal** — 1623–1662
  At age sixteen generalized Pappus's degenerate two-line Hexagon Theorem (`VII.138–139`) to an arbitrary smooth conic section in his 1640 *Essai pour les coniques* (*Hexagrammum Mysticum*).
  *Locus*: cf. II pp. 884–889.

.. _person-steiner:

**Jakob Steiner** — 1796–1863
  Proved that the six $S_3$ permutations of Pappus's hexagon generate six Pappus lines concurring three-by-three at two Steiner Star points, and extended Pascal's 60 lines into 20 Steiner points (1832).
  *Locus*: cf. II pp. 884–889.

.. _person-hultsch:

**Friedrich Otto Hultsch** — 1833–1906
  Established the definitive 3-volume Weidmann Greek text (1876–1878), apparatus criticus from *Codex Vaticanus Graecus* 218, parallel Latin translation, and *Index Graecitatis* for Pappus's *Synagoge*.
  *Edition*: Berlin 1876–1878.

.. _person-hilbert:

**David Hilbert** — 1862–1943
  Proved in *Grundlagen der Geometrie* (1899; ``geometor/hilbert``) that in an incidence plane Pappus's Hexagon Theorem holds if and only if coordinate multiplication is commutative.
  *Locus*: cf. II pp. 886–889.

.. _person-heath:

**Sir Thomas Little Heath** — 1861–1940
  Authored the comprehensive public-domain English mathematical exposition of Pappus's *Collection* in *History of Greek Mathematics* Vol. II (1921) and cross-referenced Hultsch's Pappus throughout ``geometor/euclid``.
  *Locus*: Heath 1921 II pp. 355–439.

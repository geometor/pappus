Baroulkos · Greek Witness to Hero's Mechanics Notes
===================================================

.. rubric:: Archimedes's Boast & Arabic Survival

.. folio:: 0069
   :page: 1060

Because :person:`Hero's <heron>` three-book *Mechanics* survives complete only in Qusṭā ibn Lūqā's Arabic translation, Pappus VIII.10–11 (with its engravings ``0071.g1`` and ``0074.g1``) is our most precious extant Greek witness to ancient mechanical engineering and preserves Archimedes's ``«δός μοι ποῦ στῶ καὶ κινῶ τὴν γῆν»``.

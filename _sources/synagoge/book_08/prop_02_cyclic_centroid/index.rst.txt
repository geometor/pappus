Proposition 2: Cyclic Triangle Centroid Invariance
==================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VIII, Proposition 2 (:cite:`Hultsch III, pp. 1038–1040 <hultsch:1878>`) · Critical English Edition

.. folio:: 0047
   :page: 1038

**10. (IV)** What was deferred shall now be proved. For let ΓΔ be to ΔΘ as ΓΕ is to ΕΚ, and let ΔΕ and ΘΚ be joined cutting each other at :g:`Λ <0048.g1:L>`; [we must prove] that ΘΛ is equal to ΚΛ, and that the ratio of ΔΛ to ΛΕ is compounded of that of ΔΘ to ΘΓ and that of ΓΚ to ΚΕ.

Let :g:`ΓΖ <0048.g1:/ G Z />` be drawn through :g:`Γ <0048.g1:G>` parallel to ΘΚ and meet ΔΕ produced at :g:`Ζ <0048.g1:Z>`. Since, then, ΔΛ and ΛΕ are two straight lines, and ΖΛ is outside, the ratio of ΔΛ to ΛΕ is compounded of that of ΔΛ to ΛΖ and that of ΛΖ to ΕΛ.
But the ratio of ΔΘ to ΘΓ is the same as ΔΛ to ΛΖ because :g:`ΓΖ <0048.g1:/ G Z />` is parallel to ΚΘ, and ΓΚ to ΚΕ is the same as ΖΛ to ΛΕ because triangles ΓΕΖ, ΕΚΛ are equiangular; therefore the ratio of ΔΛ to ΛΕ is compounded of ΔΘ to ΘΓ and ΓΚ to ΚΕ.
In the same way it will be shown that the ratio of ΚΛ to ΛΘ is compounded of that of ΚΕ to ΕΓ and that of ΓΔ to ΔΘ, when :g:`ΓΜ <0048.g1:/ M G />` is drawn through :g:`Γ <0048.g1:G>` parallel to ΕΔ and meets ΚΘ produced at :g:`Μ <0048.g1:M>`.
For since again ΚΛ and ΛΘ are two straight lines with ΛΜ taken outside, the ratio of ΚΛ to ΛΘ is compounded of that of ΚΛ to ΛΜ

.. folio:: 0049
   :page: 1040

and that of ΛΜ to ΛΘ. But the ratio of ΚΛ to ΛΜ is the same as that of ΚΕ to ΕΓ because ΕΔ is again parallel to :g:`ΓΜ <0048.g1:/ M G />`,
and the ratio of ΛΜ to ΛΘ is the same as that of :g:`ΓΔ <0048.g1:/ D G />` to ΔΘ because triangles ΔΘΛ and ΓΘΜ are equiangular; therefore the ratio of ΚΛ to ΛΘ is the same as that compounded of ΚΕ to ΕΓ,
that is ΔΘ to ΔΓ, and the ratio of :g:`ΓΔ <0048.g1:/ D G />` to ΔΘ, which produces the ratio of equality; therefore the ratio of ΚΛ to ΛΘ is that of equality; therefore ΚΛ is equal to ΛΘ.

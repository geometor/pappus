Book VIII · Philological & Mathematical Notes
=============================================

.. rubric:: Inclined-Plane Statics, Fixed-Aperture Constructions & Alexandrian Engineering

.. folio:: 0107
   :page: 1096

1. Pappus's Inclined Plane (Prop. 9) vs. Stevin and Galileo (*Hultsch III, pp. 1054–1058*)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Pappus's **Proposition 9** is antiquity's sole surviving geometric derivation of inclined-plane equilibrium, directly stimulating Guido Ubaldo del Monte and Galileo after :cite:`Commandino's 1588 Latin edition <commandino:1588>`.

2. Greek Witness to Hero's *Mechanics* and *Baroulkos* (*Hultsch III, pp. 1060–1073*)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Book VIII is our primary Greek witness to :person:`Hero's <heron>` :term:`Baroulkos <baroulkos>`, the five simple machines, and :person:`Archimedes's <archimedes>` famous saying ``«δός μοι ποῦ στῶ καὶ κινῶ τὴν γῆν»``.

3. From Pappus's *Heni Diastemati* to the Poncelet–Steiner Theorem (*p. 1074 ff.*)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Single-compass constructions (:term:`heni diastemati <heni-diastemati>`) inspired Abū al-Wafāʾ, Tartaglia, Ferrari, Cardano, and the 1833 Poncelet–Steiner theorem.

Notes: Proposition 23 · Seven Hexagons in a Circle
==================================================

.. rubric:: Philological & Historical Commentary

1. Analysis Replaces Blind Trial
--------------------------------

Pappus explicitly introduces Proposition 23 to illustrate how theoretical analysis frees the artisan from empirical trial-and-error (*ἀνάλογον πεῖραν διαφεύγειν*). Without analysis, an artisan fitting seven hexagons into a circle must guess the side length by repeated approximation; with analysis, the exact relationship $s_6 = R / \sqrt{7}$ yields a direct Euclidean construction.

2. The Fixed-Compass Tradition
------------------------------

This proposition belongs to the ancient tradition of constructions executed *ἑνὶ διαστήματι* (:term:`with a single compass opening <heni-diastemati>`). This problem influenced tenth-century Islamic geometry (Abū al-Wafāʾ al-Būzjānī) and Renaissance Italy (the 1547 contest between Tartaglia and Ferrari), culminating in the Poncelet–Steiner theorem of 1833.

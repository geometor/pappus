Proposition 23 · Seven Hexagons Symbolic Summary
================================================

.. rubric:: Exact Symbolic Audit over :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \sqrt{7})`

.. folio:: 0107
   :page: 1096

In :func:`geometor.pappus.construct_honeycomb_isoperimetry` with hexagon side :math:`s_6 = 2`:

* Center: :math:`H_0 = (0, 0)`.
* Rim vertex: :math:`N = (1, 3\sqrt{3})`.
* Circumradius quadrance: :math:`Q(H_0, N) = 1^2 + (3\sqrt{3})^2 = 28 = 7 \cdot 2^2 = 7 s_6^2`, giving :math:`R = 2\sqrt{7}`.

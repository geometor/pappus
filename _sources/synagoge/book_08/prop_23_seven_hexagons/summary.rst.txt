Proposition 23 · Seven Hexagons Symbolic Summary
================================================

.. rubric:: Exact Symbolic Audit over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{3}, \sqrt{7})$

.. folio:: 0107
   :page: 1096

In :func:`geometor.pappus.construct_honeycomb_isoperimetry` with hexagon side $s_6 = 2$:

* Center: $H_0 = (0, 0)$.
* Rim vertex: $N = (1, 3\sqrt{3})$.
* Circumradius quadrance: $Q(H_0, N) = 1^2 + (3\sqrt{3})^2 = 28 = 7 \cdot 2^2 = 7 s_6^2$, giving $R = 2\sqrt{7}$.

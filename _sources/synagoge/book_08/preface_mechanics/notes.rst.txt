Notes: Preface to Hermodorus · Architectonic Mechanics
======================================================

.. rubric:: Philological & Historical Commentary

1. The Defense of Archimedean Mechanics by Carpus
-------------------------------------------------

Pappus cites :person:`Carpus of Antioch <carpus>` to refute Plutarch's famous portrait (*Life of Marcellus*) claiming that Archimedes regarded mechanical construction as base and ignoble. Carpus insists that Archimedes valued mechanical inventions and that applying geometry to material arts ennobles rather than degrades mathematical science.

2. Manuscript Witness: Codex Vaticanus Graecus 218
--------------------------------------------------

Book VIII begins on folio 207r of *Codex Vaticanus Graecus 218* (reproduced in Hultsch Vol. III, p. 1022, corresponding to folio scan `0031`). The text is the primary surviving Greek transmission of Hero's mechanical taxonomy and Hellenistic mechanical theory.

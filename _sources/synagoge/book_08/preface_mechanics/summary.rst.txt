Preface to Hermodorus · Mechanics Summary
=========================================

.. rubric:: Structural Division of Alexandrian Mechanics

.. folio:: 0031
   :page: 1022

Following :person:`Hero of Alexandria <heron>`, mechanics divides into **theoretical** (*λογικόν*) and **manual** (*χειρουργικόν*), encompassing five engineering disciplines: machine-makers, engine-makers, water-lifters, automata-builders, and sphere-makers.

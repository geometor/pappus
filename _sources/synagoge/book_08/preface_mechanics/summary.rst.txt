Summary: Preface to Hermodorus · Architectonic Mechanics
========================================================

.. rubric:: Structural Division of Alexandrian Mechanics

1. Rational vs. Manual Division
-------------------------------

Following :person:`Hero of Alexandria <heron>`, mechanics is divided into:

* **Theoretical (*λογικόν*)**: Geometry, arithmetic, astronomy, and physical inquiry.
* **Manual (*χειρουργικόν*)**: Metallurgy, architecture, carpentry, painting, and practical fabrication.

The true master architect (*ἀρχιτέκτων*) unifies both domains, grounding practical engineering in rigorous geometric proof.

2. Five Specialized Mechanical Disciplines
------------------------------------------

1. **Machine-makers (*μηχανουργοί*)**: Lifting immense loads with minimal effort.
2. **Engine-makers (*ὀργανοποιοί*)**: Designing projectile artillery (ballistas, catapults).
3. **Water-lifters (*ὑδραγωγοί*)**: Raising water from depths (Archimedean screws, siphon pumps).
4. **Automata-builders (*θαυματοποιοί*)**: Pneumatic and mechanical devices creating wonder.
5. **Sphere-makers (*σφαιροποιοί*)**: Mechanical planetaria reproducing celestial rotations.

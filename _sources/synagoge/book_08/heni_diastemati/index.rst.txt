Single-Compass Constructions and Five-Point Conics
==================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VIII, Chapters 26–38 (:cite:`Hultsch III, pp. 1074–1095 <hultsch:1878>`) · Critical English Edition

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 1074

Turning to **instrumental geometry** (*τὰ ἰδίως λεγόμενα ὀργανικά*), Pappus observes that architects, stonecutters, and engineers constantly face practical constraints in the field: restoring the true diameter of a broken temple column from a surviving fragment (**Prop. 13**), constructing the conic section through five given points on a cylindrical surface (**Prop. 14**), or executing plane constructions :term:`with a single fixed compass opening <heni-diastemati>` (*τὰ ἑνὶ διαστήματι γραφόμενα*, *Hultsch III p. 1074 ff.*)—inaugurating the fixed-"rusty"-compass tradition later carried forward by Abū al-Wafāʾ, Tartaglia, Ferrari, Cardano, and Poncelet–Steiner.

Single-Compass Geometry · Islamic & Renaissance Notes
=====================================================

.. rubric:: From Pappus to Poncelet–Steiner

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 1074

Pappus's section on *τὰ ἑνὶ διαστήματι γραφόμενα* launched a 1,500-year mathematical lineage through Abū al-Wafāʾ (10th c.), Tartaglia–Ferrari–Cardano (1547), and the Poncelet–Steiner theorem (1822/1833).

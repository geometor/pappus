Planar Isoperimetric Chain · Reception & Modern Extremal Theory
===============================================================

.. rubric:: The Evolution from Zenodorus to Steiner Symmetrization

1. The Radian/Tangent Inequality in Antiquity
---------------------------------------------

In Proposition 3 (*Hultsch I, pp. 314–318*), Pappus proves the trigonometric lemma that for circular sectors, the ratio of areas exceeds the ratio of angles:

.. math::

   \frac{\tan \alpha}{\tan \beta} > \frac{\alpha}{\beta} \qquad (0 < \beta < \alpha < \pi/2).

This is the exact planar dual of Ptolemy's chord-arc inequality ($\frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin \beta} < \frac{\alpha}{\beta}$, *Almagest* I.10), illustrating the shared analytical apparatus across Hellenistic astronomy and geometry.

2. Steiner Symmetrization (1838)
--------------------------------

Jakob Steiner re-established the isoperimetric theorem in 1838 using the method of *symmetrization*—a purely synthetic procedure showing that any non-circular plane figure can be transformed into a figure of equal perimeter with strictly greater area.

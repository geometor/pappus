Proposition 2 · Circle Extremum Summary
=======================================

.. rubric:: Radius vs. Apothem Inequality

.. folio:: 0248
   :page: 1236

* Since $P_{\text{circ}} > 2\pi R$, similar scaling to perimeter $P$ yields apothem $a_n < R$, so $\frac{1}{2} P R > \frac{1}{2} P a_n$.

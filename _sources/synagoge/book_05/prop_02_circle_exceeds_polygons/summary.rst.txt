Proposition 2 · Circle Extremum Summary
=======================================

.. rubric:: Radius vs. Apothem Inequality

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0343
   :page: 310

* Since :math:`P_{\text{circ}} > 2\pi R`, similar scaling to perimeter :math:`P` yields apothem :math:`a_n < R`, so :math:`\frac{1}{2} P R > \frac{1}{2} P a_n`.

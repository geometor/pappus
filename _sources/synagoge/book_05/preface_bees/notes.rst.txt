Preface on Bees · Historical & Philosophical Commentary
=======================================================

.. rubric:: The Teleological Argument in Ancient Science

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0337
   :page: 304

:person:`Pappus <pappus>` synthesizes biological teleology with Alexandrian geometry in this preface to :person:`Megethius <megethius>` (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 304–308 <hultsch-1876>`), noting that bees choose the hexagonal cell because only equilateral triangles, squares, and hexagons tile the plane.

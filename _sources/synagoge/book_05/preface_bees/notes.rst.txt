Preface on Bees · Historical & Philosophical Commentary
=======================================================

.. rubric:: The Teleological Argument in Ancient Science

.. folio:: 0246
   :page: 1234

Pappus's preface to Megethius synthesizes Aristotle's biological teleology (*Historia Animalium* IX.40) with Alexandrian geometry, later discussed explicitly by Charles Darwin in *The Origin of Species* (Ch. VII, 1859).

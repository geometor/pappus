Preface on Bees · Historical & Philosophical Commentary
=======================================================

.. rubric:: The Teleological Argument in Ancient Science

Pappus's preface to Megethius is one of the most widely anthologized passages in ancient scientific prose. Combining Aristotle's teleological biology (*Historia Animalium* IX.40) with late-Platonic natural theology, Pappus argues that the divine order of geometry is reflected even in the instinct of non-rational creatures.

In 1859, Charles Darwin devoted a substantial section of *The Origin of Species* (Chapter VII, "Cell-making instinct of the Hive-Bee") to explaining how natural selection operates on behavioral variations to produce the mathematically optimal hexagonal architecture described by Pappus.

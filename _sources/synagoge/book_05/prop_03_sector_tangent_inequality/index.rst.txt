Proposition 3: Circular Sector Tangent Inequality
=================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book V, Prop. 3 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 314–318 <hultsch:1876>`; App. IV, p. 1237) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: 0249
   :page: 1237

In **Proposition 3**, Pappus establishes the fundamental inequality for circular sectors and right-triangle tangents that supplies the analytical engine behind Proposition 1: for $0 < \beta < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$, the ratio of tangents strictly exceeds the ratio of central angles:

.. math::

   \frac{\tan \alpha}{\tan \beta} > \frac{\alpha}{\beta}.

Comparing right-triangle areas bounded by the circumscribed circular arc and tangent intercept proves the inequality purely by sector bounds—the exact dual of Ptolemy's chord inequality ($\frac{\sin \alpha}{\sin \beta} < \frac{\alpha}{\beta}$ in *Almagest* I.10).

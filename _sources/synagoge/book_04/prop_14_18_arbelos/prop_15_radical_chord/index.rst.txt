Proposition 15: Tangent Circles and Radical Axis
================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book IV, Chs. 20–22 / Prop. 15 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 208–212 <hultsch-1876>`) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0241
   :page: 208

.. enunciation::

   **20. (15)** Let there be two circles :math:`ZB` and :math:`BM` about centers :math:`A` and :math:`\Gamma` tangent to one another at :math:`B`, and let :math:`BM` be greater,
   and let another circle :math:`K\Lambda` about center :math:`H` touch them at :math:`K` and :math:`\Lambda`, and let :math:`\Gamma H` and :math:`AH` be joined

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0243
   :page: 210

.. enunciation::

   (indeed they will fall through :math:`K` and :math:`\Lambda`), and the straight line joined through :math:`K` and :math:`\Lambda` produced will cut circle :math:`ZB`,
   and meets the straight line produced through centers :math:`A` and :math:`\Gamma` because side :math:`AK` is greater than :math:`\Gamma\Lambda` in trapezium :math:`AK\Lambda\Gamma`. Let it then meet at :math:`E`, cutting the circle at :math:`\Delta`; to prove that as :math:`AB` is to :math:`B\Gamma`, so :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`.

.. step::

   Now it is evident when :math:`\Gamma\Delta` is joined; for triangles :math:`\Gamma\Delta\Lambda` and :math:`AKH` become equiangular, having vertical angles at :math:`\Lambda` equal and sides about angles :math:`\Gamma` and :math:`H` proportional, so that alternate angles :math:`\Delta\Gamma H` and :math:`\Gamma HA` are equal, :math:`\Gamma\Delta` is parallel to :math:`AH`, and as :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, so :math:`AK` is to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`, that is, :math:`AB` to :math:`B\Gamma`.

.. step::

   **21.** And the converse is also evident. For if as :math:`AB` is to :math:`B\Gamma`, so :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, then :math:`K\Delta` is in a straight line with :math:`\Delta E`. For :math:`AK` is parallel to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`, and as :math:`AB` is to :math:`B\Gamma`, that is, as :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, so :math:`AK` is to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`;
   therefore :math:`K\Delta` is in a straight line with :math:`\Delta E`. For if the line through :math:`K, E` does not pass through :math:`\Delta`, but through :math:`\Theta`, then as :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, so :math:`AK` is to :math:`\Gamma\Theta`, which is impossible. Similarly neither will it pass outside :math:`\Delta`, cutting :math:`\Gamma\Delta`

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0245
   :page: 212

.. step::

   produced, as at :math:`N`; for again as :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, so :math:`AK` will be to :math:`\Gamma N`, which is impossible, since it is to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`.

.. step::

   Or thus. Through :math:`K` let :math:`KN` be drawn parallel to :math:`AE`, and :math:`A\Gamma NK` becomes a parallelogram, with :math:`AK` equal to :math:`\Gamma N`. And since as :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, so :math:`AK`, that is :math:`\Gamma N`, is to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`, by division as :math:`A\Gamma` is to :math:`\Gamma E`, so :math:`N\Delta` is to :math:`\Delta\Gamma`.
   Alternately, as :math:`A\Gamma`, that is :math:`KN`, is to :math:`N\Delta`, so :math:`E\Gamma` is to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`. And about the equal angles at :math:`N` and :math:`\Gamma` the sides are proportional; therefore triangle :math:`E\Delta\Gamma` is similar to triangle :math:`\Delta NK`; hence angle :math:`E\Delta\Gamma` equals angle :math:`N\Delta K`. And :math:`\Gamma N` is straight; therefore :math:`K\Delta E` is also straight.

.. step::

   **22.** I say also that the rectangle contained by :math:`KE` and :math:`E\Lambda` equals the square on :math:`EB`. For since as :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, so :math:`AB` is to :math:`B\Gamma`, that is, to :math:`\Gamma Z`, the remainder :math:`BE` will also be to the remainder :math:`EZ` as :math:`AE` is to :math:`E\Gamma`, that is, as :math:`KE` is to :math:`E\Delta`.
   But as :math:`KE` is to :math:`E\Delta`, so rectangle :math:`KE\Lambda` is to rectangle :math:`\Lambda E\cdot E\Delta`, and as :math:`BE` is to :math:`EZ`, so the square on :math:`BE` is to rectangle :math:`BEZ`, and rectangle :math:`\Lambda E\cdot E\Delta` equals rectangle :math:`BE\cdot EZ` (:just:`III.36`); therefore rectangle :math:`KE\Lambda` equals the square on :math:`EB`.

Proposition 17 · Inductive Step Summary
=======================================

.. rubric:: Integer Step Recurrence

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0251
   :page: 218

* Recurrence relation: :math:`\frac{y_{n+1}}{d_{n+1}} = \frac{y_n}{d_n} + 1`.
* Immediately proves by induction that if :math:`\frac{y_1}{d_1} = 1`, then :math:`\frac{y_n}{d_n} = n` for all :math:`n`.

Proposition 17 · Inductive Step Summary
=======================================

.. rubric:: Integer Step Recurrence

.. folio:: 0240
   :page: 1228

* Recurrence relation: $\frac{y_{n+1}}{d_{n+1}} = \frac{y_n}{d_n} + 1$.
* Immediately proves by induction that if $\frac{y_1}{d_1} = 1$, then $\frac{y_n}{d_n} = n$ for all $n$.

Proposition 17: Adjacent Circle Altitude Recurrence
===================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book IV, Ch. 25 / Prop. 17 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 220–224 <hultsch:1876>`; App. IV, p. 1228) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: 0240
   :page: 1228

.. enunciation::

   If two consecutive circles centered at $K_n$ and $K_{n+1}$ touch both nested semicircles and touch each other externally at $\Theta$, then the sum of the $n$-th altitude and its diameter stands to its diameter in the exact same ratio as the $(n+1)$-th altitude stands to the $(n+1)$-th diameter:

   .. math::

      \frac{y_n + d_n}{d_n} = \frac{y_{n+1}}{d_{n+1}} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \frac{y_{n+1}}{d_{n+1}} = \frac{y_n}{d_n} + 1.

============================================

.. rubric:: The Phrase "Arbelos" & Ancient Precedents

.. folio:: 0237

Pappus emphasizes the antiquity (*ἐν τοῖς ἀρχαίοις*) of the circle-chain theorem, pointing back to the Hellenistic tradition of :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` (*Book of Lemmas*).

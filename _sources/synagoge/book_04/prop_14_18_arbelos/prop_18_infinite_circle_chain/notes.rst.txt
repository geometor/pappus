Proposition 18 · Infinity in Ancient Synthetic Proof
====================================================

.. rubric:: Early Greek Mathematical Induction

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0257
   :page: 224

:person:`Pappus's <pappus>` closing formula ``καὶ τοῦτο συμβαῖνον ἐπὶ τὸ ἄπειρον ἀποδειχθήσεται`` (*and this will be demonstrated as occurring without end*) is one of the clearest explicit examples of **complete mathematical induction** (base step via Prop. 16, inductive transition :math:`n \to n+1` via Prop. 17) in classical antiquity, followed in Ch. 27 by the limiting straight-line boundary cases.

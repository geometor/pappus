Arbelos Circle Chain · Section Summary
======================================

.. rubric:: Five-Step Architecture of the Proof

.. folio:: 0236
   :page: 1224

1. **Prop. 14**: Enunciation of the ancient altitude law $y_n = n\,d_n$ and scope (internal/external arbelos).
2. **Prop. 15**: Radical chord power-of-a-point relation ($ZE \cdot ZP = Z\Theta^2$).
3. **Prop. 16**: Single inscribed circle base-segment altitude relation (:just:`III.36`).
4. **Prop. 17**: Adjacent circle recurrence $\frac{y_{n+1}}{d_{n+1}} = \frac{y_n}{d_n} + 1$.
5. **Prop. 18**: Complete base-case + inductive closure *ad infinitum* and focal locus ellipse.

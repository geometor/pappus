Arbelos Circle Chain · Inversive Geometry & Historical Notes
============================================================

.. rubric:: Classical vs. Modern Inversive Perspective

.. folio:: 0236
   :page: 1224

While modern geometers prove $y_n = n\,d_n$ by circular inversion centered at $A$, Pappus establishes the entire chain purely synthetically via Props. 15–17 without departing from Euclidean tangents and power-of-a-point ratios (*Elements* III.36). Modern treatments call these **Pappus–Steiner chains**.

1. Generalization Beyond Euclid I.47
------------------------------------

Whereas *Elements* I.47 requires a right angle and squares, and *Elements* VI.31 retains the right angle while generalizing to similar figures, Pappus IV.1 drops both restrictions simultaneously.

2. Clairaut's 1741 Rediscovery
------------------------------

In 1741, Alexis Clairaut included this theorem in his *Éléments de géométrie* without noting Pappus's antiquity.

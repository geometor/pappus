1. Pappus's Parallelogram Area Theorem (*Prop. 1, Hultsch I pp. 176–179*)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Given any triangle :math:`ABC` and arbitrary parallelograms :math:`ABDE` and :math:`ACFG` erected outward on sides :math:`AB` and :math:`AC`:

* Outer sides :math:`DE` and :math:`FG` intersect at apex :math:`H`.
* Transversal vector :math:`\vec{v} = A - H` defines base parallelogram :math:`BCML` on side :math:`BC` with edges :math:`BL = CM = \vec{v}`.
* **Area Invariant**: :math:`\operatorname{Area}(BCML) = \operatorname{Area}(ABDE) + \operatorname{Area}(ACFG)` holds identically across all 10 coordinates in :math:`\mathbb{Z}[x_A, y_A, x_B, y_B, x_C, y_C, u_1, v_1, u_2, v_2]`.
* When :math:`\angle BAC = 90^\circ` and erected parallelograms are squares, the theorem reduces identically to :cite:`Euclid Elements I.47 <euclid:elements>`.

2. The Ancient Arbelos Circle Chain Theorem (*Props. 14–18, Hultsch I pp. 208–232*)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Given outer semicircle of radius :math:`R = r_1 + r_2` divided into inner semicircles of radii :math:`r_1` and :math:`r_2`:

* Chain of mutually tangent circles :math:`C_n` (:math:`n = 1, 2, 3, \dots`) inscribed in the :term:`arbelos <arbelos>`.
* **Altitude Law**: :math:`y_n = n\,d_n = 2n\,r_n` for every :math:`n \ge 1`.
* **Focal Locus**: Every center :math:`K_n` lies on the fixed ellipse with foci at :math:`O_0` and :math:`O_1`, with major axis :math:`2a = R + r_1`.

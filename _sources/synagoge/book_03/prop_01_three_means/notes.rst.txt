The Three Means · Philological & Cross-Corpus Notes
===================================================

.. rubric:: Historical Reception & Projective Connections

1. The Neo-Pythagorean Medieties
--------------------------------

As :cite:`Nicomachus of Gerasa <nicomachus:introductio>` records in his *Introduction to Arithmetic* (II.22–28), the early Pythagoreans recognized only three means: arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic (originally called the *sub-contrary* mean by Archytas and Hippasus). Eudoxus and later geometers expanded this to ten medieties by permuting ratio terms.

2. Connection to Projective Harmonics in Book VII
-------------------------------------------------

The tangent $BT$ at the circle vertex $B$ meets diameter $AC$ produced at the harmonic conjugate point $T$, giving the four-point cross-ratio:

.. math::

   (A, C; D, T) = \frac{(A - D)(C - T)}{(A - T)(C - D)} = -1.

This metric construction in Book III directly anticipates the projective harmonic lemma of :doc:`Book VII Proposition 131 <../../book_07/prop_131_harmonic/index>`.

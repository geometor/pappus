1. The Neo-Pythagorean Medieties
--------------------------------

As :cite:`Nicomachus of Gerasa <nicomachus-introductio>` records in his *Introduction to Arithmetic* (II.22–28), the early Pythagoreans recognized only three means: arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic. Eudoxus and later geometers expanded this to ten medieties.

2. Connection to Projective Harmonics in Book VII
-------------------------------------------------

The tangent :math:`BT` at the circle vertex :math:`B` meets diameter :math:`AC` produced at the harmonic conjugate point :math:`T`, giving :math:`\operatorname{CR}(A, C; D, T) = -1`, directly anticipating :doc:`Book VII Proposition 131 <../../book_07/prop_131_harmonic/index>`.

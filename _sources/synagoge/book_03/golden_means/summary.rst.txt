Golden Means · Algebraic Invariants & SymPy Closure
===================================================

.. rubric:: Symbolic Verification in the Quadratic Field :math:`\mathbb{Q}(\phi)`

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0115
   :page: 82

In :func:`geometor.pappus.construct_three_means` with parameters :math:`a = 1` and :math:`b = \phi^2`:

* Arithmetic mean: :math:`A = \frac{1 + \phi^2}{2} = \frac{\phi + 2}{2}`
* Geometric mean: :math:`G = \sqrt{1 \cdot \phi^2} = \phi`
* Harmonic mean: :math:`H = \frac{2 \cdot 1 \cdot \phi^2}{1 + \phi^2} = \frac{2\phi^2}{\phi + 2} = \frac{2\phi}{\sqrt{5}}`
* Product identity: :math:`A \cdot H - G^2 = 0`
* Harmonic cross-ratio: :math:`\operatorname{CR}(A, C; D, T) = -1` identically.

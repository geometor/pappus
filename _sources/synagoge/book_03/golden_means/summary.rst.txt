Golden Means · Algebraic Invariants & SymPy Closure
===================================================

.. rubric:: Symbolic Verification in the Quadratic Field $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$

.. folio:: 0233
   :page: 1221

In :func:`geometor.pappus.construct_three_means` with parameters $a = 1$ and $b = \phi^2$:

* Arithmetic mean: $A = \frac{1 + \phi^2}{2} = \frac{\phi + 2}{2}$
* Geometric mean: $G = \sqrt{1 \cdot \phi^2} = \phi$
* Harmonic mean: $H = \frac{2 \cdot 1 \cdot \phi^2}{1 + \phi^2} = \frac{2\phi^2}{\phi + 2} = \frac{2\phi}{\sqrt{5}}$
* Product identity: $A \cdot H - G^2 = 0$
* Harmonic cross-ratio: $\operatorname{CR}(A, C; D, T) = -1$ identically.

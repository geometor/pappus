Threefold Hierarchy of Loci · Epistemological Commentary
========================================================

.. rubric:: Methodological Purity & Algebraic Degree

.. folio:: 0228
   :page: 1216

Pappus's warning against solving a problem *out of an alien genus* (*ἐξ ἀνοικείου γένους*) anticipates modern Galois field-extension classification: plane problems correspond to quadratic tower extensions $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{\cdot})$, whereas solid problems correspond to irreducible cubics and quartics solvable by intersections of conics. Pappus specifically reproves :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>` (*Conics* V) and :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` (*On Spirals*) for invoking higher instruments where elementary ones suffice.

Definitions of the Three Medieties · Neo-Pythagorean Notes
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Archytas, Eratosthenes & Nicomachus

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0101
   :page: 68

As :cite:`Nicomachus of Gerasa <nicomachus-introductio>` records in his *Introduction to Arithmetic* (II.22–28), the early Pythagoreans recognized only these three medieties (calling the harmonic the *sub-contrary* mean in music theory), before later geometers cataloged all ten forms (*Chs. 44–46*).

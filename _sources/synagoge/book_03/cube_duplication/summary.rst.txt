Cube Duplication · Summary of Mechanical Methods
================================================

.. rubric:: Equivalence to Cubic Root Extraction ($x^3 = a^2 b$)

.. folio:: 0229
   :page: 1217

Inserting $x, y$ in continued proportion $a : x = x : y = y : b$ reduces cube duplication ($b = 2a \implies x^3 = 2a^3$) to an algebraic degree-3 problem:

* **Eratosthenes**: Parallel sliding frames (*mesolabe*).
* **Nicomedes**: Conchoid-based :term:`neusis <neusis>`.
* **Hero & Pappus**: Equal-distance ruler pivoting around rectangle/semicircle vertices.

Cube Duplication · Instrumental History Notes
=============================================

.. rubric:: From Delos to Alexandrian Automata

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0089
   :page: 56

:person:`Pappus <pappus>` preserves :person:`Eratosthenes <eratosthenes>`'s famous method alongside :person:`Hero <heron>`'s and :person:`Nicomedes <nicomedes>`'s constructions, noting explicitly that although these constructions use sliding rules and pivoted gauges rather than compasses and straightedges, they achieve the *exact* cubic proportion :math:`x^3 = a^2 b` where :person:`Pandrosion <pandrosion>`'s student had offered only a flawed plane construction.

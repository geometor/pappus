Cube Duplication · Instrumental History Notes
=============================================

.. rubric:: From Delos to Alexandrian Automata

.. folio:: 0229
   :page: 1217

Pappus preserves Eratosthenes's famous method alongside Hero's and Nicomedes's constructions, noting explicitly that although these constructions use sliding rules and pivoted gauges rather than compasses and straightedges, they achieve the *exact* cubic proportion $x^3 = a^2 b$ where Pandrosion's circle student had offered only a flawed plane construction.

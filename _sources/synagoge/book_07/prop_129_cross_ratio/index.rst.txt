Proposition 129: Cross-Ratio Invariance Across Central Projection
=================================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VII, Proposition 129 / Chs. 196–197 (:cite:`Hultsch II, pp. 870–872 <hultsch-1877>`, Lemma III to Euclid's *Porisms*) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0416
   :page: 870

.. enunciation::

   **196 (Lemma III, Prop. 129).** Into three straight lines :math:`AB, \Gamma A, \Delta A` let two straight lines :math:`\Theta E, \Theta\Delta` be drawn across; to show that as the rectangle contained by :math:`\Theta E, HZ` is to that contained by :math:`\Theta H, ZE`, so is the rectangle contained by :math:`\Theta B, \Delta\Gamma` to that contained by :math:`\Theta\Delta, B\Gamma`.

Let :math:`K\Lambda` be drawn through :math:`\Theta` parallel to :math:`Z\Gamma A`, and let :math:`\Delta A, AB` meet it at points :math:`K, \Lambda`; and through :math:`\Lambda` let :math:`\Lambda M` be drawn parallel to :math:`\Delta A` and meet :math:`E\Theta` at :math:`M`.
Since, then, as :math:`EZ` is to :math:`ZA`, so is :math:`E\Theta` to :math:`\Theta\Lambda`, and as :math:`AZ` is to :math:`ZH`, so is :math:`\Theta\Lambda` to :math:`\Theta M` (for :math:`\Theta K` is in parallel with :math:`\Theta H`), by equality therefore as :math:`EZ` is to :math:`ZH`, so is :math:`E\Theta` to :math:`\Theta M`; therefore the rectangle contained by :math:`\Theta E, HZ` is equal to that contained by :math:`EZ, \Theta M`.
And take any other rectangle :math:`EZ, \Theta H`; therefore as the rectangle :math:`E\Theta, HZ` is to :math:`EZ, H\Theta`, so is :math:`EZ, \Theta M` to :math:`EZ, H\Theta`, that is :math:`\Theta M` to :math:`\Theta H`, that is :math:`\Lambda\Theta` to :math:`\Theta K`. In the same way as :math:`K\Theta` is to :math:`\Theta\Lambda`, so is :math:`\Theta\Delta, B\Gamma` to :math:`\Theta B, \Gamma\Delta`;
conversely therefore as :math:`\Lambda\Theta` is to :math:`\Theta K`, so is the rectangle

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0418
   :page: 872

:math:`\Theta B, \Gamma\Delta` to :math:`\Theta\Delta, B\Gamma`. And as :math:`\Lambda\Theta` is to :math:`\Theta K`, so was shown to be :math:`E\Theta, HZ` to :math:`EZ, H\Theta`; and therefore as :math:`E\Theta, HZ` is to :math:`EZ, H\Theta`, so is :math:`\Theta B, \Gamma\Delta` to :math:`\Theta\Delta, B\Gamma`.

**197.** And by the compound ratio thus: since the ratio of :math:`\Theta E, HZ` to :math:`\Theta H, ZE` is compounded of that which :math:`\Theta E` has to :math:`EZ` and that which :math:`ZH` has to :math:`H\Theta`, and as :math:`\Theta E` is to :math:`EZ`, so is :math:`\Theta\Lambda` to :math:`ZA`, and as :math:`ZH` is to :math:`H\Theta`, so is :math:`ZA` to :math:`\Theta K`,
therefore :math:`\Theta E, HZ` to :math:`\Theta H, EZ` is compounded of that which :math:`\Theta\Lambda` has to :math:`ZA` and that which :math:`ZA` has to :math:`\Theta K`. And the ratio compounded of :math:`\Theta\Lambda` to :math:`ZA` and :math:`ZA` to :math:`\Theta K` is the same as that of :math:`\Theta\Lambda` to :math:`\Theta K`; therefore as :math:`\Theta E, HZ` is to :math:`\Theta H, ZE`, so is :math:`\Theta\Lambda` to :math:`\Theta K`.
For the same reason also as :math:`\Theta\Delta, B\Gamma` is to :math:`\Theta B, \Gamma\Delta`, so is :math:`\Theta K` to :math:`\Theta\Lambda`; and conversely as :math:`\Theta B, \Gamma\Delta` is to :math:`\Theta\Delta, B\Gamma`, so is :math:`\Theta\Lambda` to :math:`\Theta K`. And it was also as :math:`\Theta E, ZH` is to :math:`\Theta H, ZE`, so is :math:`\Theta\Lambda` to :math:`\Theta K`;
and therefore as :math:`\Theta E, ZH` is to :math:`\Theta H, ZE`, so is :math:`\Theta B, \Gamma\Delta` to :math:`\Theta\Delta, B\Gamma`.

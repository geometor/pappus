Notes: Proposition 129 · Cross-Ratio Invariance
===============================================

.. rubric:: Philological & Projective History

1. Lemmas to Euclid's Lost *Porisms*
------------------------------------

Proposition 129 is Lemma III of the 38 lemmas Pappus provided for :cite:`Euclid's three lost books of Porisms <euclid:porisms>`. The *Porisms* were intermediate propositions between theorems and problems that investigated the conditions under which geometric loci or invariants arise.

2. Michel Chasles and the *Rapport Anharmonique*
-------------------------------------------------------

In his *Aperçu historique sur l'origine et le développement des méthodes en géométrie* (1837) and *Les trois livres de Porismes d'Euclide* (1860), Michel Chasles established that Pappus's compound ratio in Proposition 129 is the fundamental invariant of projective geometry—the *rapport anharmonique* (cross-ratio). Through the work of :person:`Girard Desargues <desargues>`, Poncelet, Möbius, and Chasles, this single Euclidean lemma grew into the bedrock of modern synthetic and algebraic projective geometry.

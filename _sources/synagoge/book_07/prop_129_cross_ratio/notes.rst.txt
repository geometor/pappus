Proposition 129 · Cross-Ratio History Notes
===========================================

.. rubric:: From Euclid's Porisms to Michel Chasles

.. folio:: 0275
   :page: 1263

Proposition 129 is Lemma III of the 38 lemmas Pappus provided for :cite:`Euclid's Porisms <euclid:porisms>`. Michel Chasles (*Aperçu historique*, 1837; *Les trois livres de Porismes d'Euclide*, 1860) recognized this compound ratio as the *rapport anharmonique* (cross-ratio) governing projective geometry.

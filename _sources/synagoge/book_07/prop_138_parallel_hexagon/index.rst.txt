Proposition 138: Pappus Hexagon on Parallel Carriers
====================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VII, Proposition 138 / Ch. 206 (:cite:`Hultsch II, p. 884 <hultsch-1877>`, Lemma XII to Euclid's *Porisms*) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0430
   :page: 884

.. picture:: /_static/constructions/pappus_parallel_steiner.svg
   :alt: Synagoge Book VII Proposition 138 — Parallel Carriers and Even Steiner Point S+ = (90/73, 38/73)

.. enunciation::

   **206 (Lemma XII, Prop. 138).** Now that these things have been demonstrated, it will be possible to show that if :math:`AB, \Gamma\Delta` be parallel, and certain straight lines :math:`A\Delta, AZ, B\Gamma, BZ` fall upon them, and :math:`E\Delta, E\Gamma` be joined, [that] the line through :math:`H, M, K` becomes straight.

For since :math:`\Delta AZ` is a triangle, and :math:`AE` is parallel to :math:`\Delta Z`, and :math:`E\Gamma` has been drawn through meeting :math:`\Delta Z` at :math:`\Gamma`, by what was written before it comes to be as :math:`\Delta Z` to :math:`Z\Gamma`, so the rectangle :math:`\Gamma E, H\Theta` to :math:`\Gamma H, \Theta E`. Again, since :math:`\Gamma BZ` is a triangle, and :math:`BE` has been drawn parallel to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`,
and :math:`\Delta E` has been drawn through meeting :math:`\Gamma Z\Delta` at :math:`\Delta`, it comes to be as :math:`\Gamma Z` to :math:`Z\Delta`, so the rectangle :math:`\Delta E, \Lambda K` to :math:`\Delta K, \Lambda E`; conversely therefore it comes to be as :math:`\Delta Z` to :math:`Z\Gamma`, so :math:`\Delta K, \Lambda E` to :math:`\Delta E, \Lambda K`. And it was also as :math:`\Delta Z` to :math:`Z\Gamma`,
so :math:`\Gamma E, H\Theta` to :math:`\Gamma H, \Theta E`; and therefore as :math:`\Gamma E, H\Theta` to :math:`\Gamma H, \Theta E`, so is :math:`\Delta K, \Lambda E` to :math:`\Delta E, K\Lambda` [it is reduced to the one before]. Since, then, into two straight lines :math:`\Gamma M\Lambda, \Delta M\Theta` two straight lines :math:`E\Gamma, E\Delta` have been drawn through,
and as :math:`\Gamma E, H\Theta` is to :math:`\Gamma H, \Theta E`, so is :math:`\Delta K, E\Lambda` to :math:`\Delta E, \Lambda K`, therefore the line through :math:`H, M, K` is straight; for this has been shown before.

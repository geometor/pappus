Proposition 139: Pappus's General Hexagon Collinearity Theorem
==============================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VII, Proposition 139 / Ch. 207 (:cite:`Hultsch II, p. 886 <hultsch-1877>`, Lemma XIII to Euclid's *Porisms*) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0432
   :page: 886

.. picture:: /_static/constructions/pappus_hexagon.svg
   :alt: Synagoge Book VII Proposition 139 — Pappus's General Intersecting-Carrier Hexagon Theorem

.. enunciation::

   **207 (Lemma XIII, Prop. 139).** But let :math:`AB, \Gamma\Delta` not be parallel, and let them meet at :math:`N`; to show again that the line through :math:`H, M, K` is straight.

Since into three straight lines :math:`AN, AZ, A\Delta` from the same point :math:`\Gamma` two straight lines :math:`\Gamma E, \Gamma\Delta` have been drawn through, it comes to be as the rectangle :math:`\Gamma E, H\Theta` to :math:`\Gamma H, \Theta E`, so the rectangle :math:`\Gamma N, Z\Delta` to :math:`N\Delta, \Gamma Z`. Again, since from the same point :math:`\Delta` into three straight lines :math:`BN, B\Gamma, BZ` two straight lines :math:`\Delta E, \Delta N` have been drawn through,
as the rectangle :math:`N\Gamma, Z\Delta` is to :math:`N\Delta, Z\Gamma`, so is :math:`\Delta K, E\Lambda` to :math:`\Delta E, K\Lambda`. Yet as :math:`N\Gamma, Z\Delta` is to :math:`N\Delta, \Gamma Z`, so was shown to be :math:`\Gamma E, H\Theta` to :math:`\Gamma H, \Theta E`; and therefore as :math:`\Gamma E, \Theta H` is to :math:`\Gamma H, \Theta E`, so is :math:`\Delta K, E\Lambda` to :math:`\Delta E, K\Lambda` [it has been reduced to what also holds for parallels]. By what was written before, therefore, the line through :math:`H, M, K` is straight.

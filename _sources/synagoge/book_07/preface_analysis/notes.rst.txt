Preface to Hermodorus · Historical & Canon Notes
================================================

.. rubric:: The Transmission of Hellenistic Higher Geometry

.. folio:: 0269
   :page: 1257

Book VII is dedicated to Pappus's son (or pupil) :person:`Hermodorus <hermodorus>`. Of the 33 books cataloged by Pappus, only Euclid's *Data* and Apollonius's *Conics* (Books I–IV in Greek, V–VII in Arabic) survive complete, making Book VII's 242 lemmas our indispensable witness to the lost works.

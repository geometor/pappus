Proposition 131: Complete-Quadrilateral Harmonic Conjugates
===========================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VII, Proposition 131 / Ch. 199 (:cite:`Hultsch II, pp. 874–876 <hultsch-1877>`, Lemma V to Euclid's *Porisms*) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0420
   :page: 874

.. picture:: /_static/constructions/pappus_harmonics.svg
   :alt: Synagoge Book VII Proposition 131 — Emergent Harmonic Ranges in the Pappus Configuration

.. enunciation::

   **199 (Lemma V, Prop. 131).** If :math:`AB\Gamma\Delta EZH\Theta` be the figure, it comes to be as :math:`A\Delta` to :math:`\Delta\Gamma`, so :math:`AB` to :math:`B\Gamma`. Let it be, then, as :math:`A\Delta` is to :math:`\Delta\Gamma`, so :math:`AB` is to :math:`B\Gamma`; to show that the line through :math:`A, H, \Theta` is straight.

Let :math:`K\Lambda` be drawn through :math:`H` parallel to :math:`A\Delta`. Since,

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0422
   :page: 876

then, as :math:`A\Delta` is to :math:`\Delta\Gamma`, so is :math:`AB` to :math:`B\Gamma`, yet as :math:`A\Delta` is to :math:`\Delta\Gamma`, so is :math:`K\Lambda` to :math:`\Lambda H`, and as :math:`AB` is to :math:`B\Gamma`, so is :math:`KH` to :math:`HM`, therefore also as :math:`K\Lambda` is to :math:`\Lambda H`, so is :math:`KH` to :math:`HM`,
and the remainder :math:`H\Lambda` to the remainder :math:`\Lambda M` is as :math:`K\Lambda` to :math:`\Lambda H`, that is as :math:`A\Delta` to :math:`\Delta\Gamma`. Alternando as :math:`A\Delta` is to :math:`H\Lambda`, so is :math:`\Gamma\Delta` to :math:`\Lambda M`, that is :math:`\Delta\Theta` to :math:`\Theta\Lambda`. And :math:`H\Lambda` is parallel to :math:`A\Delta`; therefore the line through points :math:`A, H, \Theta` is straight; for this is manifest.

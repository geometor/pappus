Notes: Proposition 131 · Harmonic Conjugates
============================================

.. rubric:: Philological & Projective Commentary

1. Harmonic Quadruples and the Complete Quadrilateral
-----------------------------------------------------

Pappus's Proposition 131 is historically the earliest recorded proof that the diagonal points of a complete quadrilateral divide the diagonals harmonically. In the nineteenth century, Karl Georg Christian von Staudt (*Geometrie der Lage*, 1847) showed that this construction allows one to define harmonic division and projectivity **without any concept of distance, metric, or ratio of lengths**—establishing projective geometry purely upon incidences of points and lines.

2. Invariance Across Central Projections
----------------------------------------

Because harmonic division is defined by a cross-ratio of $-1$, and cross-ratio is preserved by central projection (Proposition 129), every harmonic pencil of rays projects to a harmonic range on every transversal line. This harmonic property forms the bridge between Euclidean metric geometry and projective incidence axioms.

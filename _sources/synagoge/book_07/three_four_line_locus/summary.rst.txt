Three- and Multi-Line Locus · Summary
=====================================

.. rubric:: Algebraic Degree & Compound Ratios

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0221
   :page: 676

* **3-Line Locus**: :math:`d_1 \cdot d_2 = k \cdot d_3^2` (conic section).
* **4-Line Locus**: :math:`d_1 \cdot d_2 = k \cdot d_3 \cdot d_4` (conic section).
* **5- and 6-Line Loci**: Expressed as ratios of rectangular solids :math:`d_1 d_2 d_3 : k d_4 d_5` or :math:`d_1 d_2 d_3 : d_4 d_5 d_6` (cubic curves).
* **General** :math:`n`-**Line Locus**: Product ratio of oblique distances :math:`\prod_{i=1}^{k} d_i = C \prod_{j=k+1}^{n} d_j` expressed geometrically via **compound ratios** (*λόγοι συνημμένοι*), avoiding any dimension beyond three.

Pappus–Guldinus Centroid Theorem · Authenticity Notes
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Paul Guldin's Rediscovery & Vatican MS 218

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0225
   :page: 680

:person:`Paul Guldin <guldin>` published this theorem in *Centrobaryca* (1640). Although early critics questioned whether the sentence was a late scholium in :cite:`Commandino's 1588 translation <commandino-1588>` (and :person:`Hultsch <hultsch>` bracketed Chs. 41–42 in :cite:`Hultsch II, pp. 680–682 <hultsch-1877>`, folios ``hultsch_1877_vol2:0225`` and ``0227``), *Codex Vaticanus Graecus 218* (fol. 165r) transmits the Greek text. Ch. 40 concludes the multi-line locus on ``0225`` (p. 680), Chs. 41–42 present the Centroid Theorem and the closing tally of :cite:`Apollonius's Conics <apollonius-conics>` (487 theorems, 70 lemmas) on ``0227`` (p. 682), and Ch. 43 opens the lemmas to *Cutting Off of a Ratio* on ``0229`` (p. 684).

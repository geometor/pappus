Arbelos Circle Chain · Inversive Geometry & Historical Notes
============================================================

.. rubric:: Classical vs. Modern Inversive Perspective

1. Ancient Synthetic Method vs. Modern Inversion
------------------------------------------------

While modern geometers prove $y_n = n\,d_n$ almost instantaneously by applying a circular inversion centered at point $A$ (which transforms the two tangent semicircles into parallel straight lines and the chain into a stack of congruent circles between parallel lines), Pappus's proof is purely synthetic. He achieves the exact same result through three lemmas based on Euclidean tangents and power-of-a-point relations (*Elements* III.36).

2. Generalization to External Arbelos Chains
--------------------------------------------

Pappus explicitly notes (*Hultsch I, p. 208*) that the altitude property holds whether the semicircles are nested internally (the classical *arbelos*) or embrace one another externally. Modern treatments call this family the *Pappus–Steiner chains*.

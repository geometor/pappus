Arbelos Circle Chain · Inversive Geometry & Historical Notes
============================================================

.. rubric:: Classical vs. Modern Inversive Perspective

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0241
   :page: 208

While modern geometers prove :math:`y_n = n\,d_n` by circular inversion centered at :math:`A`, :person:`Pappus <pappus>` establishes the entire chain purely synthetically via Props. 15–17 without departing from Euclidean tangents and power-of-a-point ratios (*Elements* III.36). Modern treatments call these **Pappus–Steiner chains** (after :person:`Jakob Steiner <steiner>`).

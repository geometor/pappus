Proposition 17: Adjacent Circle Altitude Recurrence
===================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book IV, Ch. 25 / Prop. 17 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 218–224 <hultsch-1876>`) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0251
   :page: 218

.. enunciation::

   **25. (17)** With the same things supposed, let a circle :math:`\Theta PT` be described tangent to the original semicircles and to circle :math:`EH\Theta` at points :math:`\Theta, P, T`,
   and from centers :math:`A` and :math:`\Pi` let perpendiculars :math:`AM` and :math:`\Pi N` be drawn to base :math:`B\Gamma`; I say that as :math:`AM` together with the diameter of circle :math:`EH` is to its diameter, so :math:`\Pi N` is to the diameter of circle :math:`\Theta PT`.

.. step::

   Let :math:`BZ` be drawn at right angles to :math:`B\Delta`; therefore it touches semicircle :math:`BH\Gamma`. And let :math:`A\Pi` joined be produced to :math:`Z`. Since by what was proved before, as the sum of

   :page: 220

.. step::

   :math:`\Gamma B\Delta` is to their difference :math:`\Gamma\Delta`, so :math:`BM` in the first figure is to the radius of circle :math:`EH\Theta`,
   and in the second and third figures as their difference is to their sum, that is, as the difference of :math:`\Gamma B` and :math:`B\Delta` is to :math:`\Gamma\Delta`, so :math:`MB` is to the radius of circle :math:`EH\Theta` and :math:`BN` is to the radius of circle :math:`\Theta PT`, therefore alternately as :math:`MB` is to :math:`BN`, so radius :math:`A\Theta` of circle :math:`EH\Theta` is to

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0255
   :page: 222

.. step::

   radius :math:`\Theta\Pi` of circle :math:`\Theta PT`. But as :math:`MB` is to :math:`BN`, so :math:`AZ` is to :math:`Z\Pi` (for when :math:`ZM` is joined, as :math:`MB` is to :math:`BN`, so :math:`MZ` will be to :math:`Z\Xi`). And therefore as :math:`AZ` is to :math:`Z\Pi`, so radius :math:`A\Theta` of circle :math:`EH\Theta` is to radius :math:`\Theta\Pi` of circle :math:`\Theta PT`.
   And a circle :math:`BPE\Delta` touches circles :math:`EH\Theta` and :math:`P\Theta T` at points :math:`P` and :math:`E`; therefore by Proposition 15 proved above, the straight line joining points :math:`P` and :math:`E` produced will fall upon point :math:`Z`, and rectangle :math:`EZP` will equal the square on :math:`\Theta Z`.
   But rectangle :math:`EZP` also equals the square on :math:`ZB`; therefore the square on :math:`ZB` equals the square on :math:`Z\Theta`, so :math:`BZ` equals :math:`Z\Theta`. And since :math:`MA` produced cuts the circumference of circle :math:`EH\Theta` at :math:`\Sigma`, while :math:`\Pi N` cuts the circumference of circle :math:`\Theta PT` at :math:`O`,
   therefore :math:`A\Theta` equals :math:`A\Sigma` and :math:`\Pi O` equals :math:`\Pi\Theta`, and the line joining :math:`O, \Sigma` will pass through :math:`\Theta`; for alternate angles :math:`\Theta A\Sigma` and :math:`\Theta\Pi O` are equal, triangle :math:`A\Theta\Sigma` is equiangular with triangle :math:`\Pi\Theta O`, and :math:`A\Pi` is straight; therefore the line drawn through :math:`\Sigma, \Theta, O` is also straight.
   And it will also pass through :math:`B`; for :math:`\Theta OB` is straight because as :math:`BZ` is to :math:`Z\Theta`, so :math:`O\Pi` is to :math:`\Pi\Theta`, since angles :math:`BZ\Theta` and :math:`O\Pi\Theta` are equal in parallels :math:`BZ` and :math:`O\Pi`;

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0257
   :page: 224

.. step::

   for this was proved before in Proposition 15.
   And let :math:`B\Pi` joined be produced and meet :math:`MA` produced at :math:`K`. Since therefore as :math:`MB` is to :math:`BN`, that is, as :math:`KB` is to :math:`B\Pi`, so :math:`AZ` is to :math:`Z\Pi` and :math:`A\Theta` is to :math:`\Theta\Pi`, as :math:`KB` is to :math:`B\Pi`, so also :math:`A\Sigma` is to :math:`\Pi O` and :math:`\Sigma K` is to :math:`\Pi O`; therefore :math:`A\Sigma` equals :math:`\Sigma K`.
   Since then the whole :math:`AK` equals the whole diameter of circle :math:`EH\Theta`, and as :math:`KM` is to :math:`K\Sigma`, so :math:`N\Pi` is to :math:`O\Pi`, also as :math:`MK` is to :math:`KA`, that is, as :math:`MA` together with the diameter of circle :math:`EH\Theta` is to the diameter, so :math:`N\Pi` is to the diameter of circle :math:`\Theta PT`, as was to be proved.

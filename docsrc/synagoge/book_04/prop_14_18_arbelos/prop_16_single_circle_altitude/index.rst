Proposition 16: Altitude Relation for an Inscribed Circle
=========================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book IV, Lemma 2 / Prop. 16 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 214–220 <hultsch:1876>`; App. IV, p. 1227) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: 0239
   :page: 1227

.. enunciation::

   For any single circle inscribed tangent to two nested semicircles upon baseline $AC$, the square of the perpendicular height of its center above the baseline stands in a fixed rectangle relation to the base segments cut off by the rays joining the contact points to the outer semicircle's endpoints (:just:`III.36`).

This metric invariant yields the exact base case $y_1 = d_1$ when combined with the third semicircle tangency condition.

Proposition 18 · Infinity in Ancient Synthetic Proof
====================================================

.. rubric:: Early Greek Mathematical Induction

.. folio:: 0241
   :page: 1229

Pappus's closing formula ``καὶ τοῦτο συμβαῖνον ἐπὶ τὸ ἄπειρον ἀποδειχθήσεται`` (*and this will be demonstrated occurring to infinity*) is one of the clearest explicit examples of **complete mathematical induction** (base step via Prop. 16, inductive transition $n \to n+1$ via Prop. 17) in classical antiquity.

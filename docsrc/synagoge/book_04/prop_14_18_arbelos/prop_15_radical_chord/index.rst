Proposition 15: Tangent Circles and Radical Axis
================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book IV, Lemma 1 / Prop. 15 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 210–214 <hultsch:1876>`; App. IV, p. 1226) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: 0238
   :page: 1226

.. enunciation::

   If two circles touch at $\Theta$ and a third circle touches both at $E$ and $P$, the chord $EP$ produced meets the line of centers on the common tangent at $Z$ where $ZE \cdot ZP = Z\Theta^2$ (the power of the homothety/inversion pole).

1. The Lost Preface to Book IV
------------------------------

In *Codex Vaticanus Graecus* 218, Book IV begins abruptly with Proposition 1 without an address to Hermodorus or Pandrosion. Philologists (:cite:`Hultsch 1876 <hultsch:1876>`, Vol. I, p. 176) conclude that the introductory folio was lost prior to the 10th century.

2. Pappus's Theorem and the Shear Principle
-------------------------------------------

Pappus's proof of Proposition 1 relies entirely on Euclid's shear proposition (*Elements* I.35). By applying shear twice on each side through intermediate parallelograms :math:`AB\text{-}HA` and :math:`AC\text{-}HA`, the areas transfer without metric computation.

3. The Shoemaker's Knife in Antiquity
-------------------------------------

The term *arbelos* (*ἄρβηλος*, shoemaker's knife) appears first in the :person:`Archimedean <archimedes>` tradition preserved by Thābit ibn Qurra (*Liber Assumptorum*, Props. 4–6). Pappus attributes the circle chain proposition to "the ancients" (*οἱ ἀρχαῖοι*), indicating an early Hellenistic origin.

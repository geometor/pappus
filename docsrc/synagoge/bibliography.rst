Bibliography & Primary Sources
==============================

Critical register of **100% public-domain** primary editions, Renaissance and modern translations, classical treatises summarized in the *Synagoge*, and projective descendants. Every ``:cite:`` reference in the Codex resolves directly to its anchor below.

Ancient Mathematical & Mechanical Treatises in the *Synagoge*
-------------------------------------------------------------

.. _cite-euclid-elements:

**Euclid** — *Elements (Στοιχεῖα, Books I–XIII)* (Alexandria, c. 300 BCE; ``geometor/euclid`` & ``geometor/elements``) [c. 300 BCE]
  Key: ``euclid:elements``
  Axiomatic foundation generalized and supplemented across Pappus's *Synagoge* (`IV.1` generalizing `I.47` & `VI.31`; `III.5–16` extending `VI.13`; `III.54–58` complementing `XIII.13–18`).

.. _cite-euclid-data:

**Euclid** — *Data (Δεδομένα)* (First treatise of the *Treasury of Analysis*, *Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II pp. 636–638) [c. 300 BCE]
  Key: ``euclid:data``
  90 propositions on magnitudes given in magnitude, ratio, species, or position; heads Pappus's 33-book analytical curriculum.

.. _cite-euclid-porisms:

**Euclid** — *Porisms (Πορίσματα, 3 books — lost)* (apud Pappum, *Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II pp. 648–660 & Lemmas `VII.127–164`, pp. 866–918) [c. 300 BCE]
  Key: ``euclid:porisms``
  Lost three-book masterpiece of 171 propositions in 29 genera; Pappus's 38 lemmas to the *Porisms* include Cross-Ratio Invariance (`VII.129`), Harmonic Division (`VII.131`), and the Hexagon Theorems (`VII.138–139`).

.. _cite-euclid-surface-loci:

**Euclid** — *Surface Loci (Τόποι πρὸς ἐπιφανείᾳ, 2 books — lost)* (apud Pappum, *Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II p. 636 & Lemmas `VII.235–242`, pp. 1004–1014) [c. 300 BCE]
  Key: ``euclid:surface-loci``
  Lost two-book treatise whose final Pappus lemmas (`VII.238`, Hultsch II p. 1008) preserve the earliest surviving proof of the Focus–Directrix eccentricity property of conic sections.

.. _cite-apollonius-conics:

**Apollonius of Perga** — *Conics (Κωνικά, 8 books)* (*Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II pp. 672–682 & Lemmas `VII.165–234`, pp. 918–1004) [c. 200 BCE]
  Key: ``apollonius:conics``
  Culmination of solid-locus geometry in the *Treasury of Analysis*; discussed by Pappus in connection with the Three- and Four-Line Locus and the Pappus–Guldinus Centroid Theorem.

.. _cite-apollonius-cutting-off-ratio:

**Apollonius of Perga** — *Cutting Off of a Ratio, Cutting Off of an Area, Determinate Section, Tangencies, Inclinations & Plane Loci (12 books)* (apud Pappum, *Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II pp. 636, 640–672 & Lemmas `VII.1–126`, pp. 684–866) [c. 200 BCE]
  Key: ``apollonius:cutting-off-ratio``
  Six two-book Apollonian analytical treatises forming the central trunk of Pappus's *Treasury of Analysis*.

.. _cite-aristaeus-solid-loci:

**Aristaeus the Elder** — *Five Books of Solid Loci (Τόποι στερεοί, 5 books — lost)* (apud Pappum, *Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II pp. 636, 670–678) [fl. c. 320 BCE]
  Key: ``aristaeus:solid-loci``
  Pre-Apollonian treatise on conic sections connected with the Three- and Four-Line Locus problem.

.. _cite-archimedes-method-spirals:

**Archimedes of Syracuse** — *On Spirals, Book of Lemmas (Arbelos), Semi-Regular Polyhedra & Equilibria of Planes* (apud Pappum, *Synagoge* IV pp. 208–245, V pp. 352–358, VIII p. 1060) [3rd cent. BCE]
  Key: ``archimedes:method-spirals``
  Primary source for Pappus's arbelos circle chain (`IV.14–18`), spiral quadrature, complete table of all 13 Archimedean semi-regular solids (`V.19`), and center-of-gravity mechanics (`VIII`).

.. _cite-nicomachus-introductio:

**Nicomachus of Gerasa** — *Introduction to Arithmetic (Ἀριθμητικὴ Εἰσαγωγή, Book II)* (*Synagoge* III, Hultsch I pp. 68–105) [c. 100 CE]
  Key: ``nicomachus:introductio``
  Source for the ten Pythagorean and Neo-Pythagorean medieties (*μεσότητες*) synthesized geometrically by Pappus in Book III.

.. _cite-zenodorus-isoperimetric:

**Zenodorus** — *On Isoperimetric Figures (Περὶ ἰσοπεριμέτρων σχημάτων — fragments)* (apud Pappum, *Synagoge* V, Hultsch I pp. 308–350) [2nd cent. BCE]
  Key: ``zenodorus:isoperimetric``
  Hellenistic treatise on planar and spatial isoperimetric inequalities reworked and sharpened by Pappus in Book V following his Preface on the Honeycomb.

.. _cite-heron-mechanica:

**Hero of Alexandria** — *Mechanics (Μηχανικά) & Baroulkos (Βαρουλκός)* (apud Pappum, *Synagoge* VIII, Hultsch III pp. 1022–1073, 1104–1115) [1st cent. CE]
  Key: ``heron:mechanica``
  Principal Greek source for the five simple machines, toothed gear-trains, worm screw, and weight-hauling reduction preserved in Greek through Pappus Book VIII.

.. _cite-proclus-in-euclidem:

**Proclus Diadochus** — *In Primum Euclidis Elementorum Librum Commentarii* (ed. Friedlein 1873; ``geometor/proclus``) [c. 470 CE]
  Key: ``proclus:in-euclidem``
  Companion GEOMETOR codex preserving Pappus's symmetry proof of *Elements* `I.5` and axiomatic remarks, while paralleling *Synagoge* VII on geometric analysis and synthesis.

Modern Analytic & Projective Descendants
----------------------------------------

.. _cite-descartes-geometrie:

**René Descartes** — *La Géométrie* (Leiden, 1637, Book I: The Pappus Locus Problem) [1637]
  Key: ``descartes:geometrie``
  Inaugurated analytic coordinate geometry specifically to solve the generalized $n$-line Pappus locus posed in *Synagoge* VII (`Hultsch II, pp. 676–680`) via Commandino's 1588 Latin edition.

.. _cite-pascal-essai-coniques:

**Blaise Pascal** — *Essai pour les coniques (Hexagrammum Mysticum)* (Paris, February 1640) [1640]
  Key: ``pascal:essai-coniques``
  Generalized Pappus's two-line Hexagon Theorem (*Synagoge* `VII.138–139`) to any smooth conic section, yielding 60 Pascal lines for six conic points.

.. _cite-steiner-1832:

**Jakob Steiner** — *Systematische Entwickelung der Abhängigkeit geometrischer Gestalten von einander* (Berlin: Fincke, 1832) [1832]
  Key: ``steiner:1832``
  Established cross-ratio projectivity and proved that the six $S_3$ Pappus lines concur in two triples at the Steiner Star points $(S_+, S_-)$.

.. _cite-hilbert-1899:

**David Hilbert** — *Grundlagen der Geometrie* (Leipzig: Teubner, 1899, Ch. V–VI; ``geometor/hilbert``) [1899]
  Key: ``hilbert:1899``
  Demonstrated in ``geometor/hilbert`` that Pappus's Hexagon Theorem (*Pascalischer Satz* for two lines) is algebraically equivalent to commutativity of multiplication in the coordinate division ring.

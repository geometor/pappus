Three- and Multi-Line Locus · Summary
=====================================

.. rubric:: Algebraic Degree & Compound Ratios

.. folio:: 0270
   :page: 1258

* **3-Line Locus**: $d_1 \cdot d_2 = k \cdot d_3^2$ (conic section).
* **4-Line Locus**: $d_1 \cdot d_2 = k \cdot d_3 \cdot d_4$ (conic section).
* **$n$-Line Locus**: Product ratio of distances $\prod_{i=1}^k d_i = C \prod_{j=k+1}^{n} d_j$ expressed geometrically via **compound ratios** (*λόγοι συγκείμενοι*).

Multi-Line Locus · Historical Notes on Descartes
================================================

.. rubric:: The Birth of Analytic Geometry (1637)

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0221
   :page: 676

In Book I of :cite:`La Géométrie (1637) <descartes-geometrie>`, :person:`René Descartes <descartes>` took Pappus's statement of the :math:`n`-line locus problem (read through :cite:`Commandino's 1588 Latin edition <commandino-1588>`) as the benchmark test for his coordinate method, showing that the :math:`n`-line locus defines algebraic curves of degree :math:`\lceil n/2 \rceil`.

Hultsch (*Vol. II, pp. 676–680*, folios ``hultsch_1877_vol2:0221``, ``0223``, and ``0225``) brackets Ch. 34–35's polemical excursus and Ch. 37's marginal remark as suspected interpolations, while preserving Chs. 36 and 38–40 as the canonical Hellenistic statement of the problem.

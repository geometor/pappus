Multi-Line Locus · Historical Notes on Descartes
================================================

.. rubric:: The Birth of Analytic Geometry (1637)

.. folio:: 0270
   :page: 1258

In Book I of :cite:`La Géométrie (1637) <descartes:geometrie>`, :person:`René Descartes <descartes>` took Pappus's statement of the $n$-line locus problem as the benchmark test for his coordinate method, showing that the $n$-line locus defines algebraic curves of degree $\lceil n/2 \rceil$.

1. Blaise Pascal and the Conic Generalization (1640)
----------------------------------------------------

In his 1640 broadside :cite:`Essay pour les coniques <pascal:essai-coniques>`, :person:`Blaise Pascal <pascal>` generalized Pappus's two-line pair to any smooth conic (*Hexagrammum Mysticum*).

2. David Hilbert and Field Commutativity (1899)
-----------------------------------------------

In :cite:`Grundlagen der Geometrie <hilbert:1899>`, :person:`David Hilbert <hilbert>` proved that Pappus VII.139 holds in a Desarguesian projective plane if and only if multiplication in the coordinate skew-field is commutative ($a \cdot b = b \cdot a$). Plates ``0415.g1`` and ``0415.g2`` illustrate the Porisms quadrilateral taxonomy (*hyption* and *paryption*) from Hultsch Vol. III's *Index Graecitatis*.

Pappus–Guldinus Centroid Theorem · Authenticity Notes
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Paul Guldin's Rediscovery & Vatican MS 218

.. folio:: 0271
   :page: 1259

:person:`Paul Guldin <guldin>` published this theorem in *Centrobaryca* (1640). Although early critics questioned whether the sentence was a late scholium in :cite:`Commandino's 1588 translation <commandino:1588>`, *Codex Vaticanus Graecus 218* (fol. 165r) confirms its Greek authenticity (*Hultsch II, p. 682*).

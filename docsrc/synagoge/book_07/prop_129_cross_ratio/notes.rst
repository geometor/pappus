Proposition 129 · Cross-Ratio History Notes
===========================================

.. rubric:: From Euclid's Porisms to Michel Chasles

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0416
   :page: 870

Proposition 129 (Hultsch Chs. 196–197, :cite:`Hultsch II, pp. 870–872 <hultsch-1877>`, Greek folios ``hultsch_1877_vol2:0416`` and ``0418``) is Lemma III of the 38 lemmas Pappus provided for :cite:`Euclid's Porisms <euclid-porisms>`. Pappus supplies two proofs: a first demonstration (Ch. 196) eliminating the auxiliary rectangle :math:`EZ \cdot H\Theta`, and an alternative proof (Ch. 197) directly via compound ratios (*διὰ τοῦ συνημμένου*). Michel Chasles (*Aperçu historique*, 1837; *Les trois livres de Porismes d'Euclide*, 1860) recognized this ratio as the *rapport anharmonique* (cross-ratio) governing projective geometry.

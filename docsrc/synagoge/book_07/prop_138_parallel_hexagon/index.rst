Proposition 138: Pappus Hexagon on Parallel Carriers
====================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VII, Proposition 138 (:cite:`Hultsch II, pp. 884–886 <hultsch:1877>`, Lemma XII to Euclid's *Porisms*) · Critical English Edition

In **Proposition 138**, Pappus proves the collinearity of opposite cross-joins for a hexagon whose vertices lie alternately on two **parallel lines** $a \parallel b$.

.. picture:: /_static/constructions/pappus_parallel_steiner.svg
   :alt: Synagoge Book VII Proposition 138 — Parallel Carriers and Even Steiner Point S+ = (90/73, 38/73)

.. enunciation::

   **Proposition 138 (Pappus Hexagon on Parallel Lines)**: If two straight lines :g:`A0A2 <g8:[ A0 A2 ]>` and :g:`B0B2 <g8:[ B0 B2 ]>` are parallel, and upon them points :g:`A0 <g8:A0>`, :g:`A1 <g8:A1>`, :g:`A2 <g8:A2>` and :g:`B0 <g8:B0>`, :g:`B1 <g8:B1>`, :g:`B2 <g8:B2>` be taken, and their cross-joins :g:`A0B1 <g8:[ A0 B1 ]>` & :g:`A1B0 <g8:[ A1 B0 ]>`, :g:`A0B2 <g8:[ A0 B2 ]>` & :g:`A2B0 <g8:[ A2 B0 ]>`, :g:`A1B2 <g8:[ A1 B2 ]>` & :g:`A2B1 <g8:[ A2 B1 ]>` meet in points :g:`M01 <g8:M01>`, :g:`M20 <g8:M20>`, and :g:`M12 <g8:M12>`, then these three points **lie on a single straight line** parallel to the original carrier lines.

.. step::

   **Demonstration**: By the similarity of alternate triangles formed by transversal rays cutting parallel lines $a \parallel b$, the height of each intersection point $M_{ij}$ above line $a$ is uniquely determined by the ratio of segments on the two parallel lines:

   .. math::

      \frac{y(M_{ij})}{h - y(M_{ij})} = \frac{|A_i - A_j|}{|B_j - B_i|}.

.. step::

   Through cross-multiplication of proportional segments, the vertical distance of all three intersection points from carrier line $a$ is identical, proving that the Pappus line is not merely straight, but is itself parallel to the two carriers.

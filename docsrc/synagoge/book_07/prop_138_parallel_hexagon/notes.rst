Notes: Proposition 138 · Parallel Carriers
==========================================

.. rubric:: Philological & Projective Commentary

1. The Parallel Case as an Affine Theorem
-----------------------------------------

In Euclid's *Porisms*, Proposition 138 served as the foundational lemma when the lines of interest did not converge within the finite plane. In modern terms, it is an affine theorem that holds in any affine plane where the Theorem of Desargues is valid.

2. Connection to Hessenberg's Theorem
-------------------------------------

In 1905, Gerhard Hessenberg proved the remarkable foundational result that **Pappus's Theorem implies Desargues's Theorem** in any projective plane. Proposition 138 represents the special affine case where the axis of perspectivity is the line at infinity, bridging classical Euclidean similarity geometry with modern axiomatic geometry.

Proposition 138 · Affine Limit & Hessenberg Notes
=================================================

.. rubric:: Affine Translation Planes

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0430
   :page: 884

Prop. 138 (Hultsch Ch. 206, :cite:`Hultsch II, p. 884 <hultsch-1877>`, Greek folio ``hultsch_1877_vol2:0430``; Lemma XII to :cite:`Euclid's Porisms <euclid-porisms>`) represents the affine limit (:math:`\ell_\infty`) of Prop. 139, standing at the center of Gerhard Hessenberg's 1905 proof that Pappus's theorem implies Desargues's theorem in any projective plane. In Hultsch's Greek text, lines 12–13 omit the explicit intersection :math:`M` of :math:`A\Delta` and :math:`B\Gamma` (supplied in the facing Latin from Simson), and line 26 brackets the scholium referring back to Lemma X.

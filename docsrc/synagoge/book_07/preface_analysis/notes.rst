Preface to Hermodorus · Historical & Canon Notes
================================================

.. rubric:: The Transmission of Hellenistic Higher Geometry

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0179
   :page: 634

Book VII is dedicated to Pappus's son (or pupil) :person:`Hermodorus <hermodorus>` (:cite:`Hultsch II, pp. 634–636 <hultsch-1877>`, folios ``hultsch_1877_vol2:0179`` and ``hultsch_1877_vol2:0181``). Of the 33 books cataloged by Pappus, only :cite:`Euclid's Data <euclid-data>` and :cite:`Apollonius's Conics <apollonius-conics>` (Books I–IV in Greek, V–VII in Arabic) survive complete, making Book VII's 242 lemmas our indispensable witness to the lost works.

In Ch. 2 (*Hultsch II, p. 636*, lines 15–16), :person:`Hultsch <hultsch>` brackets the definition of :term:`diorismos <diorismos>` as a later glossator's interpolation, though the term itself is central to Pappus's inventory of the analytical treatises.

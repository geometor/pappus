Preface to Hermodorus · Historical & Canon Notes
================================================

.. rubric:: The Transmission of Hellenistic Higher Geometry

1. Hermodorus and Alexandrian Instruction
-----------------------------------------

Book VII is dedicated to Pappus's son (or pupil) :person:`Hermodorus <hermodorus>`. While Book III was an polemical critique directed to Pandrosion's circle, Book VII is a comprehensive graduate guide to the highest mathematical corpus of the ancient world.

2. Survival of the 33 Books
---------------------------

Of the 33 books listed by Pappus:

* Only Euclid's *Data* and Apollonius's *Conics* (Books I–IV in Greek, Books V–VII in Arabic) survive in their entirety.
* Apollonius's *Cutting Off of a Ratio* survives solely in Arabic translation (discovered and edited by Edmond Halley in 1706).
* All other 20 books (Euclid's *Porisms* and *Surface Loci*, Aristaeus's *Solid Loci*, Apollonius's *Tangencies*, *Plane Loci*, *Determinate Section*, and *Inclinations*, and Eratosthenes's *On Means*) have been lost to posterity except for the extensive lemmas and lemmas preserved here in Book VII.

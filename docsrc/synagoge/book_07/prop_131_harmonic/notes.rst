Proposition 131 · Non-Metric Projective Notes
=============================================

.. rubric:: Karl von Staudt & Metric-Free Projective Geometry

.. folio:: hultsch_1877_vol2:0420
   :page: 874

Pappus VII.131 (Hultsch Ch. 199, :cite:`Hultsch II, pp. 874–876 <hultsch-1877>`, Greek folios ``hultsch_1877_vol2:0420`` and ``0422``) is Lemma V to :cite:`Euclid's Porisms <euclid-porisms>` and the earliest recorded proof that the harmonic division :math:`A\Delta : \Delta\Gamma = AB : B\Gamma` on a complete quadrilateral yields collinear diagonal points :math:`A, H, \Theta`—used by Karl von Staudt (*Geometrie der Lage*, 1847) to found projective geometry without metric axioms.

Proposition 131 · Non-Metric Projective Notes
=============================================

.. rubric:: Karl von Staudt & Metric-Free Projective Geometry

.. folio:: 0276
   :page: 1264

Pappus VII.131 is the earliest recorded proof that the diagonal points of a complete quadrilateral divide the diagonals harmonically—used by Karl von Staudt (*Geometrie der Lage*, 1847) to found projective geometry without any metric axioms.

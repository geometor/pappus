1. Euclid's Lost *Porisms*
--------------------------

Of all the lost works of antiquity, Euclid's three books of *Porisms* (*Πορίσματα*) have stimulated the greatest mathematical detective work (Fermat, Halley, Simson, Chasles). Book VII of the *Synagoge* is our primary surviving source, preserving 38 preparatory lemmas.

2. Hilbert's Projective Axiomatics (1899)
-----------------------------------------

In *Grundlagen der Geometrie* (1899), :person:`David Hilbert <hilbert>` proved that Pappus's Theorem (Prop. 139) is equivalent to the **commutativity of multiplication** in the coordinate skew-field of a projective geometry.

3. The Descartes Connection
---------------------------

In 1637, :person:`René Descartes <descartes>` took the multi-line locus problem described by Pappus (*Hultsch II, pp. 676–680*) as the explicit test case for coordinate geometry in *La Géométrie*.

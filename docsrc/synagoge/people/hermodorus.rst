Hermodorus
==========

**Hermodorus** (*Ἑρμόδωρος*) — fl. c. 340 CE
  Son of Pappus (*ὦ τέκνον Ἑρμόδωρε*) and dedicatee of both *Synagoge* Book VII (*Treasury of Analysis*) and Book VIII (*Mechanics*).
  *Hultsch loci*: II p. 634, 3; III p. 1022, 3.

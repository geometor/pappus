Zenodorus
=========

**Zenodorus** (*Ζηνόδωρος*) — 2nd cent. BCE
  Author of *On Isoperimetric Figures*; proved that among isoperimetric :math:`n`-gons the regular one is greatest and the circle exceeds every regular polygon—recast and extended by Pappus in Book V.
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 308–350.

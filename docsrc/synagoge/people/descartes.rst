Descartes
=========

**René Descartes** — 1596–1650
  Made Pappus's Three-, Four-, and $n$-Line Locus problem (*Synagoge* VII, Hultsch II pp. 676–680) the centerpiece of his 1637 *La Géométrie*.
  *Locus*: cf. II pp. 676–680.

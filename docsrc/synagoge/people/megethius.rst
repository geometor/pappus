Megethius
=========

**Megethius** (*Μεγέθιος*) — fl. c. 335 CE
  Dedicatee of *Synagoge* Book V (*On Isoperimetric Figures*), addressed in the celebrated Preface on the geometrical forethought of bees.
  *Hultsch loci*: I p. 304, 2.

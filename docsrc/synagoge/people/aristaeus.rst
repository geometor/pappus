Aristaeus
=========

**Aristaeus the Elder** (*Ἀρισταῖος ὁ πρεσβύτερος*) — fl. c. 320 BCE
  Elder contemporary of Euclid who authored *Five Books of Solid Loci* (conic sections) in the *Treasury of Analysis* and compared the five regular polyhedra.
  *Hultsch loci*: I p. 132; II pp. 636, 672–678.

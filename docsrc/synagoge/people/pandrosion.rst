Pandrosion
==========

**Pandrosion of Alexandria** (*Πανδροσίων*) — fl. c. 330 CE
  Alexandrian woman mathematician and teacher of geometry, dedicatee of *Synagoge* Book III (*ὦ κρατίστη Πανδροσίων*, restored by Hultsch from feminine vocative manuscript readings where Commandino had assumed masculine).
  *Hultsch loci*: I p. 30, 3; pp. 30–175.

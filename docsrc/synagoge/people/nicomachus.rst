Nicomachus
==========

**Nicomachus of Gerasa** (*Νικόμαχος ὁ Γερασηνός*) — c. 60–120 CE
  Neo-Pythagorean arithmetician who classified ten numerical medieties in his *Introduction to Arithmetic*, cited by Pappus in Book III (pp. 68–70).
  *Hultsch loci*: I pp. 68–85.

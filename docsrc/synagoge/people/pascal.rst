Pascal
======

**Blaise Pascal** — 1623–1662
  At age sixteen generalized Pappus's degenerate two-line Hexagon Theorem (`VII.138–139`) to an arbitrary smooth conic section in his 1640 *Essai pour les coniques* (*Hexagrammum Mysticum*).
  *Locus*: cf. II pp. 884–889.

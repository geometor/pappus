Proposition 2: Circle Exceeds Isoperimetric Regular Polygons
============================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book V, Ch. 5 / Prop. 2 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 310–312 <hultsch-1876>`) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0343
   :page: 310

.. enunciation::

   **5. 2.** Again, let :math:`AB\Gamma` be an equilateral and equiangular polygon having its perimeter equal to the circumference of circle :math:`\Delta EZ`; I say that circle :math:`\Delta EZ` is greater than polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`.

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0345
   :page: 312

.. step::

   Let :math:`\Theta` be taken as the center of circle :math:`\Delta EZ`, and :math:`H` as the center of the circle circumscribed about polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`, and about the circle let there be circumscribed a polygon :math:`\Lambda MN\Xi O` similar to :math:`AB\Gamma`, let :math:`\Theta\Delta` be joined, and from :math:`H` let perpendicular :math:`HK` be drawn to :math:`A\Gamma`.
   Since, then, the perimeter of polygon :math:`\Lambda MN\Xi O` is greater than the circumference of circle :math:`\Delta EZ`, as is assumed by :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` in *On the Sphere and Cylinder* [because it encloses it], while the circumference of the circle equals the perimeter of polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`,
   the perimeter of polygon :math:`\Lambda MN\Xi O` is also greater than the perimeter of polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`. And the polygons are similar; therefore :math:`\Lambda\Delta` is greater than :math:`AK`. And triangle :math:`AHK` is similar to triangle :math:`\Lambda\Theta\Delta` (for the whole polygons are similar); therefore :math:`\Theta\Delta` is also greater than :math:`HK`.
   [And the circumference of circle :math:`\Delta EZ` equals the perimeter of polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`;] therefore the rectangle contained by :math:`\Delta\Theta` and the circumference of circle :math:`\Delta EZ` is greater than the rectangle contained by :math:`HK` and the perimeter of polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`.
   And the rectangle contained by :math:`\Delta\Theta` and the circumference of the circle is double circle :math:`\Delta EZ` (for this too was proved by :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` in *Measurement of a Circle*), while the rectangle contained by :math:`HK` and the perimeter of polygon :math:`AB\Gamma` is double polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`; taking halves, circle :math:`\Delta EZ` is greater than polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`.

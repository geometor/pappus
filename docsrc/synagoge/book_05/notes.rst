1. Transmission of Zenodorus's Lost Treatise
--------------------------------------------

Pappus's treatment of isoperimetric figures in Book V draws upon the lost treatise *On Isoperimetric Figures* by :cite:`Zenodorus <zenodorus:isoperimetric>` (c. 200–140 BCE), preserved through Pappus Book V and Theon's *Almagest* commentary.

2. The Thirteen Semi-Regular Archimedean Solids
-----------------------------------------------

In Chapter 34 / Prop. 19 (*Hultsch I, pp. 352–358*), Pappus preserves the **sole surviving ancient Greek catalogue of all thirteen Archimedean semi-regular polyhedra**.

3. The Modern Honeycomb Theorem
-------------------------------

Hales (1999) proved the complete Honeycomb Conjecture celebrated by Pappus.

Proposition 1: Isoperimetric Regular Polygons
=============================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book V, Ch. 4 / Prop. 1 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 308–310 <hultsch-1876>`) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0341
   :page: 308

.. picture:: /_static/constructions/honeycomb_isoperimetry.svg
   :alt: Synagoge Book V Proposition 1 & Book VIII Proposition 23 — Equal-Perimeter Polygons (P = 12) and Seven-Cell Honeycomb Rosette

.. enunciation::

   **4. 1.** Let :math:`AB\Gamma` and :math:`\Delta EZ` be two equilateral and equiangular polygons, and let their perimeters be equal, and let :math:`\Delta EZ` have the greater number of angles; I say that :math:`\Delta EZ` is greater than :math:`\Gamma AB`.

.. step::

   For let :math:`H` and :math:`\Theta` be taken as the centers of the circles circumscribed about them, let perpendiculars :math:`HK` and :math:`\Theta\Lambda` be drawn, and let :math:`AH`, :math:`H\Gamma`, :math:`\Theta\Delta`, and :math:`\Theta Z` be joined. Since :math:`E\Delta Z` has more angles than :math:`AB\Gamma`,
   :math:`\Delta Z` measures the perimeter of polygon :math:`\Delta EZ` more times than :math:`A\Gamma` measures that of :math:`AB\Gamma`. Therefore :math:`A\Gamma` is greater than :math:`\Delta Z` (for the perimeters are supposed equal), so that :math:`AK` is also greater than :math:`\Delta\Lambda` (each being half of its respective side).
   Let :math:`KM` be placed equal to :math:`\Delta\Lambda`, and let :math:`MH` be joined. And since whatever part the straight line :math:`A\Gamma` is of the perimeter of polygon :math:`AB\Gamma`, that same part is the angle :math:`AH\Gamma` of four right angles (because the polygon is equilateral),
   and likewise whatever part :math:`\Delta Z` is of the perimeter of :math:`\Delta EZ`, that same part is angle :math:`\Delta\Theta Z` of four right angles, and the perimeters are equal to each other and four right angles to four right angles, as :math:`A\Gamma` is to the perimeter of :math:`AB\Gamma`, so is angle :math:`H` to four right angles;
   and as the perimeter of :math:`\Delta EZ`, that is, of :math:`AB\Gamma`, is to :math:`\Delta Z`, so are four right angles to angle :math:`\Delta\Theta Z`.

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0343
   :page: 310

.. step::

   *Ex aequali*, therefore, as :math:`A\Gamma` is to :math:`\Delta Z`, so is :math:`\angle AH\Gamma` to :math:`\angle \Delta\Theta Z`;
   and therefore as :math:`AK` is to :math:`\Delta\Lambda`, that is, to :math:`KM`, so is :math:`\angle AHK` to :math:`\angle \Delta\Theta\Lambda`. Now :math:`AK` has to :math:`KM` a greater ratio than :math:`\angle AHK` has to :math:`\angle MHK` (for this has been proved in the Lemmas on Spherics);
   therefore :math:`\angle AHK` also has to :math:`\angle \Delta\Theta\Lambda` a greater ratio than :math:`\angle AHK` has to :math:`\angle MHK`; therefore :math:`\angle MHK` is greater than :math:`\angle \Delta\Theta\Lambda`. And the right angle at :math:`K` equals the right angle at :math:`\Lambda`; therefore the remaining angle :math:`\angle HMK` is less than :math:`\angle \Theta\Delta\Lambda`.
   Let :math:`\angle KMN` be constructed equal to :math:`\angle \Theta\Delta\Lambda`; and :math:`\Delta\Lambda` equals :math:`KM`; therefore :math:`\Lambda\Theta` equals :math:`KN`; therefore :math:`\Theta\Lambda` is greater than :math:`KH`. And the perimeters are equal;
   therefore the rectangle contained by :math:`\Lambda\Theta` and the perimeter of :math:`\Delta EZ` is greater than the rectangle contained by :math:`KH` and the perimeter of :math:`AB\Gamma`. And the polygons are halves of the aforesaid regions; therefore polygon :math:`\Delta EZ` is greater than :math:`AB\Gamma`.
   [For the altitude is equal since the perimeter of the two rectilinear figures is the same, and the bases :math:`\Theta\Lambda` and :math:`HK` are unequal, and as base :math:`\Theta\Lambda` is to base :math:`HK`, so is the parallelogram under :math:`\Theta\Lambda` and the perimeter of :math:`\Delta EZ` to the parallelogram under :math:`HK` and the perimeter of :math:`AB\Gamma`; and their halves, the polygons, are unequal, so that :math:`\Delta EZ` is greater than :math:`AB\Gamma`.]

Proposition 6: Maximum-Area Polygon Is Equilateral
==================================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book V, Props. 5–6 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 322–326 <hultsch:1876>`; App. IV, p. 1238) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: 0250
   :page: 1238

.. enunciation::

   Of all rectilinear polygons having an equal perimeter $P$ and the same number of sides $n$, any polygon that is **not equilateral** cannot attain maximum area; replacing any unequal adjacent pair of sides by an isosceles vertex on the same diagonal strictly increases area (:doc:`Proposition 4 <../prop_04_isosceles_extremum/index>`).

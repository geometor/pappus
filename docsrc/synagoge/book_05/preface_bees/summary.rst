Preface on Bees · Tessellation Invariants Summary
=================================================

.. rubric:: Vertex Angle Diophantine Analysis

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0337
   :page: 304

For regular polygons of :math:`n` sides to tile around a common vertex, :math:`k \frac{(n-2)\times 180^\circ}{n} = 360^\circ \iff (n-2)(k-2) = 4`, yielding only triangles (:math:`n=3, k=6`), squares (:math:`n=4, k=4`), and hexagons (:math:`n=6, k=3`).

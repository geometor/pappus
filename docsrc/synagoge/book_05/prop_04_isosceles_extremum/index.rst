Proposition 4: Isosceles Triangle Area Extremum
===============================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book V, Prop. 4 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 318–322 <hultsch:1876>`; App. IV, p. 1237) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: 0249
   :page: 1237

.. enunciation::

   Among all triangles standing upon a common base $AC$ and having equal sum of the remaining two sides ($AB + BC = AZ + ZC$), the **isosceles** triangle ($AZ = ZC$) encloses strictly the **greatest area**.

Extending the shorter side and comparing perpendicular altitudes reduces the area comparison directly to the triangle inequality.

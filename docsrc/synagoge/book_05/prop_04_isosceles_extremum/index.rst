Proposition 4: Isosceles Triangle Area Extremum
===============================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book V, Ch. 11 / Prop. 5 (:cite:`Hultsch I, pp. 318–322 <hultsch-1876>`) · Original English Translation

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0351
   :page: 318

.. enunciation::

   **11. 5.** Among :term:`isoperimetric <isoperimetros>` triangles having the same base, the isosceles triangle is greatest, and the more isosceles is always greater.

.. step::

   For upon base :math:`B\Gamma` let there be isoperimetric triangles, :math:`AB\Gamma` being isosceles, and :math:`B\Delta\Gamma` more isosceles than :math:`BE\Gamma` (for it is possible to construct this by what was just proved above); I say that :math:`AB\Gamma` is greatest, and :math:`B\Delta\Gamma` is greater than :math:`BE\Gamma`.

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0353
   :page: 320

.. step::

   Let :math:`BA` be produced, let :math:`AZ` be placed equal to :math:`\Gamma A`, and let :math:`Z\Delta` and :math:`\Delta A` be joined. Since :math:`Z\Delta` and :math:`\Delta B` are greater than :math:`BZ`, they are also greater than :math:`BA` and :math:`A\Gamma` (for :math:`A\Gamma` equals :math:`AZ`).
   But :math:`BA` and :math:`A\Gamma` equal :math:`B\Delta` and :math:`\Delta\Gamma`; therefore :math:`B\Delta` and :math:`\Delta Z` are greater than :math:`B\Delta` and :math:`\Delta\Gamma`. Subtracting the common segment :math:`B\Delta`, the remainder :math:`Z\Delta` is greater than :math:`\Delta\Gamma`.
   Now the two lines :math:`ZA` and :math:`A\Delta` equal the two lines :math:`\Gamma A` and :math:`A\Delta`, each to each, and base :math:`Z\Delta` is greater than base :math:`\Delta\Gamma`; therefore :math:`\angle ZA\Delta` is greater than :math:`\angle \Delta A\Gamma` (:just:`I.25`); therefore :math:`\angle ZA\Gamma` is more than double :math:`\angle \Delta A\Gamma`.
   And it is double :math:`\angle AB\Gamma`, that is, :math:`\angle A\Gamma B` (for the triangle is isosceles); therefore :math:`\angle A\Gamma B` is greater than :math:`\angle \Delta A\Gamma`. Let :math:`\angle \Gamma AH` be constructed equal to it; therefore :math:`AH` is parallel to :math:`B\Gamma` because the alternate angles are equal (:just:`I.27`).
   Hence, when :math:`\Gamma\Delta` is produced to :math:`H` and :math:`BH` is joined, it is manifest that triangle :math:`AB\Gamma` is greater than :math:`B\Delta\Gamma`; for :math:`BA\Gamma` equals :math:`BH\Gamma` (:just:`I.37`).

.. step::

   Again, let :math:`B\Delta` be produced to :math:`K`, let :math:`\Delta K` be placed equal to :math:`\Delta\Gamma`, and let :math:`KE` and :math:`\Delta E` be joined.
   Since :math:`BE` and :math:`EK` are greater than :math:`BK`, that is, than :math:`B\Delta` and :math:`\Delta\Gamma`, that is, than :math:`BE` and :math:`E\Gamma`, subtracting the common segment :math:`BE`, the remainder :math:`EK` is greater than :math:`E\Gamma`. Now the two lines :math:`K\Delta` and :math:`\Delta E` equal the two lines :math:`\Gamma\Delta` and :math:`\Delta E`, each to each,
   and base :math:`KE` is greater than base :math:`E\Gamma`; therefore :math:`\angle K\Delta E` is greater than :math:`\angle \Gamma\Delta E`; therefore :math:`\angle K\Delta\Gamma` is more than double :math:`\angle \Gamma\Delta E`. And the same angle :math:`\angle K\Delta\Gamma` is less than double :math:`\angle \Delta\Gamma B` (for :math:`\angle \Delta\Gamma B` is greater than :math:`\angle \Delta B\Gamma`;
   for :math:`\angle AB\Gamma` and :math:`\angle A\Gamma B` are equal); therefore :math:`\angle \Delta\Gamma B` is greater than :math:`\angle \Gamma\Delta E`. Let :math:`\angle \Gamma\Delta\Lambda` be constructed on :math:`\Delta\Gamma` at :math:`\Delta` equal to :math:`\angle \Delta\Gamma B`;

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0355
   :page: 322

.. step::

   for it is manifest that :math:`\Delta\Lambda` will fall between :math:`\Delta E` and :math:`\Delta K`,
   being parallel to :math:`B\Gamma` because the alternate angles are equal (:just:`I.27`). When :math:`\Gamma E` is produced to meet parallel :math:`\Delta\Lambda` at :math:`\Lambda` and :math:`B\Lambda` is joined, triangle :math:`B\Gamma\Delta` will equal :math:`B\Lambda\Gamma` [upon the same base :math:`\Gamma B` and in the parallels :math:`B\Gamma` and :math:`\Delta\Lambda`], so that triangle :math:`AB\Gamma` is greater than :math:`BE\Gamma`, while :math:`B\Delta\Gamma` is less than :math:`AB\Gamma`.

Single-Compass Geometry · Islamic & Renaissance Notes
=====================================================

.. rubric:: From :person:`Pappus <pappus>` to Poncelet–:person:`Steiner <steiner>` (:cite:`Hultsch III, pp. 1072–1096 <hultsch-1878>`)

.. folio:: 0083
   :page: 1074

:person:`Pappus <pappus>` launches the :term:`single-compass <heni-diastemati>` tradition (Ch. 27) and uses :person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`'s :cite:`Conics <apollonius-conics>` for five-point ellipses (Props. 14–16), anticipating Renaissance rusty-compass constructions and :person:`Steiner <steiner>`'s projective theorem (:cite:`Steiner 1832 <steiner-1832>`).

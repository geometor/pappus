.. rubric:: Historical & Philological Commentary

1. Pappus and the Ancient Problem of the Inclined Plane
-------------------------------------------------------

Pappus VIII.9 represents antiquity's sole surviving attempt to solve the inclined plane. In treating the weight as a sphere whose center overhangs the downhill contact, Pappus compounded horizontal sliding traction with an overhanging lever arm. While incorrect compared to the frictionless modern law $F = W\sin\alpha$ (discovered by Stevin and Galileo), Pappus's analysis spurred Renaissance mathematicians (Commandino, Guidobaldo del Monte, Galileo) to develop the correct laws of vector decomposition and virtual work.

2. Archimedes and Hero's *Baroulkos*
---------------------------------------------

Pappus preserves the famous Doric phrase attributed to Archimedes: ``«δός μοι ποῦ στῶ καὶ κινῶ τὴν γῆν»`` ("Give me a place to stand and I will move the earth"). The Baroulkos (weight-lifter) described by :person:`Hero of Alexandria <heron>` combines spur gearing with a non-reversing worm screw, illustrating how Hellenistic engineers conquered friction and achieved massive mechanical advantage.

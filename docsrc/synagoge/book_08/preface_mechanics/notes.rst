Preface to Hermodorus · Carpus & Vatican MS 218 Notes
=====================================================

.. rubric:: Philological & Historical Commentary

.. folio:: 0031
   :page: 1022

Pappus cites :person:`Carpus of Antioch <carpus>` to refute Plutarch's claim (*Life of Marcellus*) that Archimedes despised practical mechanics. Book VIII begins on folio 207r of *Codex Vaticanus Graecus 218* (Hultsch Vol. III, p. 1022, folio ``0031``).

Proposition 3: Similar Centroid Triangles
=========================================

.. rubric:: *Synagoge* Book VIII, Propositions 3–4 (:cite:`Hultsch III, pp. 1040–1048 <hultsch:1878>`) · Critical English Edition

.. folio:: 0049
   :page: 1040

In **Propositions 3–4**, Pappus establishes proportional centroid displacement laws across similar triangles arranged between parallel bases, proving that corresponding centroids preserve parallel collinearity ratios.

1. Pandrosion of Alexandria
---------------------------

The addressee of Book III, :person:`Pandrosion <pandrosion>`, is addressed in *Codex Vaticanus Graecus* 218 (f. 1r) as ``ὦ κρατίστη Πανδροσίων``. :cite:`Federico Commandino <commandino:1588>` emended ``κρατίστη`` to masculine ``κράτιστε``, whereas :cite:`Friedrich Hultsch <hultsch:1876>` restored the manuscript's feminine form, confirming Pandrosion as a leading fourth-century woman mathematician in Alexandria.

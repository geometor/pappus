Definitions of the Three Medieties · Summary
============================================

.. rubric:: Characteristic Difference Ratios

.. folio:: 0230
   :page: 1218

* Arithmetic: $\frac{a - A}{A - b} = \frac{a}{a} \implies A = \frac{a + b}{2}$.
* Geometric: $\frac{a - G}{G - b} = \frac{a}{G} \implies G = \sqrt{ab}$.
* Harmonic: $\frac{a - H}{H - b} = \frac{a}{b} \implies H = \frac{2ab}{a + b}$.

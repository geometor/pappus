Definitions of the Three Medieties · Summary
============================================

.. rubric:: Characteristic Difference Ratios

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0101
   :page: 68

* Arithmetic: :math:`\frac{a - A}{A - b} = \frac{a}{a} \implies A = \frac{a + b}{2}`.
* Geometric: :math:`\frac{a - G}{G - b} = \frac{a}{G} \implies G = \sqrt{ab}`.
* Harmonic: :math:`\frac{a - H}{H - b} = \frac{a}{b} \implies H = \frac{2ab}{a + b}`.

Cube Duplication · Summary of Mechanical Methods
================================================

.. rubric:: Equivalence to Cubic Root Extraction (:math:`x^3 = a^2 b`)

.. folio:: hultsch_1876_vol1:0089
   :page: 56

Inserting :math:`x, y` in continued proportion :math:`a : x = x : y = y : b` reduces cube duplication (:math:`b = 2a \implies x^3 = 2a^3`) to an algebraic degree-3 problem:

* :person:`Eratosthenes <eratosthenes>`: Parallel sliding frames (*mesolabe*).
* :person:`Nicomedes <nicomedes>`: Conchoid-based :term:`neusis <neusis>`.
* :person:`Hero <heron>` & :person:`Pappus <pappus>`: Equal-distance ruler pivoting around rectangle/semicircle vertices.

Theorems
========

Structured symbolic explorations tracing Pappus's fourth-century Alexandrian *Synagoge* across
both its projective incidence canon (cross-ratios, complete-quadrilateral harmonic conjugates,
Pappus's Hexagon, Jakob Steiner's six-line configuration, Golden Ratio perspectivities, and
Blaise Pascal's 1640 *Hexagrammum Mysticum*) and its classical Books III, IV, V, and VIII
constructions (semicircle means, parallelogram areas, *arbelos* circle chains, and honeycomb
isoperimetry).

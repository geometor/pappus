Theorems
========

Structured symbolic explorations tracing projective incidence from Pappus's fourth-century
Alexandrian *Collection* through Jakob Steiner's six-line configuration, complete-quadrilateral
harmonic conjugates, Golden Ratio perspectivities, and Blaise Pascal's 1640 *Hexagrammum Mysticum*.

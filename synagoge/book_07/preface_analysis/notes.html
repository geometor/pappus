Preface to Hermodorus · Historical & Canon Notes¶

The Transmission of Hellenistic Higher Geometry

Book VII is dedicated to Pappus’s son (or pupil) Hermodorus. Of the 33 books cataloged by Pappus, only Euclid’s Data and Apollonius’s Conics (Books I–IV in Greek, V–VII in Arabic) survive complete, making Book VII’s 242 lemmas our indispensable witness to the lost works.

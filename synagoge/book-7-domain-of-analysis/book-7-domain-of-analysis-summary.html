Book VII · Summary & Exact Symbolic Audit (Analysis, Cross-Ratio & Hexagon)¶

Structural Argument Map & geometor.pappus Symbolic Verification

1. Epistemological & Analytical Pillars (Hultsch II, pp. 634–688)¶

  • Analysis ($\alpha\nu\alpha\lambda\upsilon\sigma\iota\varsigma$) vs. Synthesis ($\sigma\upsilon\nu\theta\varepsilon\sigma\iota\varsigma$): Backward heuristic resolution from the assumed target to first principles vs. forward deductive composition; split into theoretical (zetetic) and problematic (poristic + diorismos).

  • The 33-Book Curriculum: 1 book Data, 12 books of Apollonian ratio/area/tangency/inclination/plane-locus treatises, 3 books Porisms, 8 books Conics, 5 books Solid Loci, 2 books Surface Loci, and 2 books On Means.

  • $n$-Line Locus & Centroid Rule: Compound-ratio formulation of the $n$-line conic/algebraic locus (pp. 676–680) and the Pappus–Guldinus Centroid Theorem $V = 2\pi\bar{r}\,A$, $S = 2\pi\bar{r}\,L$ (p. 682).

2. Projective Invariants & Straightedge Constructions (Props. 129, 131, 138–139)¶

  • Cross-Ratio Perspectivity (VII.129) — geometor.pappus.cross_ratio() and geometor.pappus.project_point() preserve $\operatorname{CR}(A, B; C, D) = \frac{(A - C)(B - D)}{(A - D)(B - C)}$ identically across central projections.

  • Complete Quadrilateral Fourth Harmonic (VII.131) — geometor.pappus.construct_harmonic_fourth() constructs D from A, B, C by straightedge alone so $\operatorname{CR}(A, B; C, D) = -1$, independent of the auxiliary quadrilateral chosen.

  • Hexagon Collinearity (VII.138–139) — geometor.pappus.construct_pappus_hexagon() constructs $M_{01}, M_{20}, M_{12}$ on the Pappus line $p$, while geometor.pappus.verify_pappus_symbolic() proves that the homogeneous $3 \times 3$ bracket determinant $\det[M_{01}, M_{12}, M_{20}]$ vanishes identically over $\mathbb{Z}[a_0, a_1, a_2, b_0, b_1, b_2, u_x, u_y]$.

  • Six-Permutation Steiner Web ($S_3$) — geometor.pappus.construct_pappus_configuration() verifies across all $6$ permutations that the $3$ even Pappus lines ($A_3$) concur at $S_+$ and the $3$ odd Pappus lines concur at $S_-$.

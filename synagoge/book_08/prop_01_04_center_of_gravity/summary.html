Summary: Props. 1–12 · Center of Gravity, Inclined Plane & Baroulkos¶

Structural & Mechanical Invariants

1. Centroid Invariance (Props. 1–4)¶

  • Definition: A suspension point such that the body remains at rest in any orientation without tilting.

  • Triangle Centroid: Concurrency of medians dividing each in ratio $2 : 1$.

  • Cyclic Centroid Invariance (Prop. 2): Let $D, E, F$ divide sides $BC, CA, AB$ of $\triangle ABC$ in equal cyclic ratio $\lambda : (1 - \lambda)$. Then $\triangle DEF$ and $\triangle ABC$ share the identical center of gravity:

    \[G_{DEF} = \frac{D + E + F}{3} = \frac{\lambda B + (1-\lambda)C + \lambda C + (1-\lambda)A + \lambda A + (1-\lambda)B}{3} = \frac{A + B + C}{3} = G_{ABC}.\]

2. Mechanical Advantage of the Baroulkos (Props. 10–11)¶

  • Input Power: $P = 5$ talents.

  • Burden Moved: $W = 1{,}000$ talents.

  • Gear Ratios: Three pairs of wheel/axle drums ($5:1$, $5:1$, $4:1$) yield $5 \times 5 \times 4 = 100 : 1$.

  • Worm Gear Advantage: Single-start endless screw doubles mechanical advantage to $200 : 1$, allowing $5$ talents to hold and lift $1{,}000$ talents with zero back-drive.

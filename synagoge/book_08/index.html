Book VIII · Architectonic Mechanics & Inscribed Hexagons¶

Μαθηματικὴ Συναγωγή Η΄ — Pappus of Alexandria, ed. Friedrich Hultsch (Vol. III, pp. 1022–1102)

Note

Edition Track (English): Complete critical English edition of Book VIII’s foundational mechanical treatises: the Preface to Hermodorus on the division of mechanics and the definition of the center of gravity, the inclined plane and Hero’s Baroulkos gear-train (Props. 1–12), and the single-compass construction of seven congruent regular hexagons inscribed in a circle (Prop. 23). Switch tracks above for Greek (Hultsch 1878), Summary, or Notes.

PAPPI ALEXANDRINI COLLECTIONIS LIBER OCTAVUS

Author: Pappus of Alexandria (fl. c. 320–340 CE)
Addressee: Hermodorus
Critical Greek Edition: Friedrich Hultsch (Vol. III, Berlin: Weidmann, 1878, pp. 1022–1174)

“The science of mechanics, my son Hermodorus, being serviceable for many great matters in human life, has rightly been deemed worthy of the highest esteem by philosophers, and is zealously pursued by nearly all who devote themselves to mathematical learning.”

—Pappus of Alexandria, Synagoge Book VIII, Chapter 1

Proposition 23 (Hultsch III, pp. 1096–1102): Into a given circle (H0, N) of center H0 and radius H0N $= R$, to inscribe seven congruent regular hexagons—one central hexagon V1..V6 concentric with the circle, and six surrounding hexagons C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6 erected outward upon the six sides of the central hexagon so that each surrounding hexagon’s opposite outer side has both endpoints fitted cleanly upon the circumference of circle (H0, N).

Cross-Ratios, Harmonic Conjugates & Golden Projectivity

Why does a purely straightedge construction preserve quantitative algebraic ratios when distances themselves are distorted by perspective projection? The answer already appears in Proposition 129 of Book VII of Pappus’s Synagoge: while lengths and simple ratios change under central projection from an apex $O$, the compound ratio of ratios—today called the projective cross-ratio—is an exact projective invariant.

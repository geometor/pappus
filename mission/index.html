Mission¶

geometor.pappus investigates the projective architecture of geometry—where straightedge incidence alone generates algebraic invariants, harmonic divisions, and golden proportions.

Core Objectives¶

  1. Exact Symbolic Incidence in $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$: Model Pappus of Alexandria’s fourth-century lemmas (Synagoge Book VII, Props. 129, 131, 138, 139) as exact directed acyclic graphs in geometor.model—never approximating a point, line, or cross-ratio in floating-point arithmetic.

  2. Combinatorial & Group-Theoretic Structure: Trace the full action of the symmetric group $S_3$ on collinear triples, uncovering the 18 cross-join meets, the six Pappus lines, the parity split into $A_3$ and $S_3 \setminus A_3$, and Jakob Steiner’s two Star concurrency points $(S_+, S_-)$.

  3. Harmonic & Golden Discovery: Unite projective cross-ratios $(A, B; C, D)$, complete-quadrilateral fourth harmonic conjugates, Wildberger rational trigonometry (quadrance and spread), and geometor.divine golden-section scanning across $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$.

  4. Interactive Visual Pedagogy: Publish dark/light-aware interactive svg.euclid-graphic diagrams paired with synchronized :g: and .. step:: walkthroughs so every incidence claim can be inspected visually and verified algebraically.

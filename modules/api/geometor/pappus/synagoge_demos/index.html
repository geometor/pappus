geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos¶

Exact symbolic Synagoge constructions for Books III, IV, and V of Pappus of Alexandria.

Brings Pappus’s classical geometric, proportional, and isoperimetric propositions alive in 100% exact SymPy and geometor.model arithmetic alongside the projective Book VII constructions in geometor.pappus.constructions:

  1. Book III, Props. 5–16 (Hultsch I, pp. 68–84) — The Three Classical Means in a Single Semicircle: exhibits the arithmetic mean $A = \frac{a+b}{2}$, geometric mean $G = \sqrt{ab}$, and harmonic mean $H = \frac{2ab}{a+b}$ in one semicircle on $AC = a + b$, proves $A \cdot H = G^2$ and $A \ge G \ge H$, and links the tangent meet $T$ to Book VII’s harmonic division $(A, C; D, T) = -1$.

  2. Book IV, Prop. 1 (Hultsch I, pp. 176–179) — Pappus’s Area Theorem: generalizes Euclid Elements I.47 from squares on a right triangle to arbitrary outward parallelograms erected on two sides of any triangle $\triangle ABC$, including a universal 10-variable polynomial proof over $\mathbb{Z}$.

  3. Book IV, Props. 14–18 (Hultsch I, pp. 208–227) — The Infinite Arbelos Circle Chain («ἀρχαία πρότασις»): constructs the shoemaker’s knife (arbelos) and its tangent circle chain $C_1, \dots, C_n$ in 100% exact rational coordinates over $\mathbb{Q}(r_1, r_2)$, proving pairwise tangencies and $y_n = n d_n = 2 n r_n$.

  4. Book V, Preface & Prop. 1 (Hultsch I, pp. 304–310) — The Sagacity of Bees & Hexagonal Honeycomb Isoperimetry: proves that $n \in \{3, 4, 6\}$ are the unique regular plane-tiling polygons, computes exact area ratios $\operatorname{Area}_6 : \operatorname{Area}_4 : \operatorname{Area}_3 = 6\sqrt{3} : 9 : 4\sqrt{3}$ for equal perimeter $P$, and builds a 7-cell hexagonal honeycomb cluster that simultaneously realizes Book VIII, Prop. 23’s 7-hexagon circle inscription.

geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos.construct_three_means(model_or_a: geometor.model.Model | ScalarInput = 9, a_or_b: ScalarInput = 16, b: ScalarInput | None = None, *, model: geometor.model.Model | None = None, include_harmonic_tangent: bool = True) → ThreeMeansResult¶

Construct Pappus’s semicircle diagram exhibiting the three classical means (Synagoge III.5–16).

Supports both construct_three_means(a, b, *, model=None) and construct_three_means(model, a, b).

On diameter $AC = a + b$ with center $O = \left(\frac{a+b}{2}, 0\right)$ and interior point $D = (a, 0)$:

  • Erect $DB \perp AC$ to the semicircle at $B = (a, \sqrt{ab})$ so \(|DB| = \sqrt{ab} = G\).

  • Join radius $OB$ so \(|OB| = \frac{a+b}{2} = A\).

  • Drop $DF \perp OB$ to foot $F$ so right-triangle similarity $\triangle BFD \sim \triangle BDO$ gives \(|BF| = \frac{|DB|^2}{|OB|} = \frac{2ab}{a+b} = H\).

  • When $a \ne b$ and include_harmonic_tangent=True, construct the circle tangent at $B$ meeting $AC$ at $T = \left(\frac{a(a+b)}{a-b}, 0\right)$, exhibiting the exact harmonic conjugate $(A, C; D, T) = -1$ that bridges Book III’s mean theory to Book VII’s Porisms.

Parameters:

  • model_or_a – Either a Model or the first positive scalar $a$.

  • a_or_b – First scalar $a$ (when model_or_a is a Model) or second scalar $b$.

  • b – Second scalar $b$ when model_or_a is a Model.

  • model – Optional explicit Model keyword argument.

  • include_harmonic_tangent – If True and $a \ne b$, construct tangent meet $T$ on $AC$.

Returns:

Populated ThreeMeansResult.

geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos.construct_pappus_area_theorem(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None, *, preset: str = 'scalene', triangle: tuple[PointInput, PointInput, PointInput] | None = None, vec_ab: PointInput | None = None, vec_ac: PointInput | None = None) → PappusAreaResult¶

Construct Pappus’s Area Theorem (Synagoge Book IV, Prop. 1, Hultsch I, pp. 176–179).

Given an arbitrary counter-clockwise triangle $\triangle ABC$ and two arbitrary outward parallelograms $ABDE$ (with $D = B + \vec{u}$, $E = A + \vec{u}$) and $ACFG$ (with $F = C + \vec{v}$, $G = A + \vec{v}$):

  1. Extend outer sides $[DE]$ and $[FG]$ to meet at $H$.

  2. Join $[HA]$ and extend it across base $[BC]$ to meet $[BC]$ at $J$.

  3. Translate segment $HA$ to $BL$ and $CM$ ($L = B + (A - H)$, $M = C + (A - H)$) forming outward parallelogram $BCML$ on base $BC$.

  4. By shear-invariance through parallels $[DE] \parallel [AB]$, $[FG] \parallel [AC]$, and $[HA] \parallel [BL] \parallel [CM]$:

    \[\operatorname{Area}(ABDE) = \operatorname{Area}(ABLH\text{-shear}) = \operatorname{Area}(BLNJ), \quad \operatorname{Area}(ACFG) = \operatorname{Area}(JCMN),\]

    whence $\operatorname{Area}(BCML) = \operatorname{Area}(ABDE) + \operatorname{Area}(ACFG)$.

Presets:

  • "scalene": Oblique scalene $\triangle ABC$ with $A = (3, 4)$, $B = (0, 0)$, $C = (7, 0)$ and skewed non-rectangular parallelograms of areas $13$ and $15$, yielding $\operatorname{Area}(BCML) = 28$.

  • "pythagoras": Exact $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle at apex $A = \left(\frac{9}{5}, \frac{12}{5}\right)$ on hypotenuse $B = (0, 0)$, $C = (5, 0)$ with squares of areas $9$ and $16$, yielding the exact $5 \times 5$ hypotenuse square of area $25$ (Elements I.47).

Parameters:

  • model – Optional Model; created if None.

  • preset – "scalene" or "pythagoras" when triangle is None.

  • triangle – Optional custom (A, B, C) counter-clockwise vertices.

  • vec_ab – Optional outward side vector $\vec{u}$ for parallelogram $ABDE$.

  • vec_ac – Optional outward side vector $\vec{v}$ for parallelogram $ACFG$.

Returns:

Populated PappusAreaResult.

geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos.construct_pappus_arbelos_chain(model_or_r1: geometor.model.Model | ScalarInput = 2, r1_or_r2: ScalarInput = 1, r2_or_count: ScalarInput | int = 4, count: int | None = None, *, model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → ArbelosChainResult¶

Construct Pappus’s Arbelos tangent circle chain (Synagoge IV.14–18, Hultsch I, pp. 208–227).

Supports both construct_pappus_arbelos_chain(r1=2, r2=1, count=4, *, model=None) and construct_pappus_arbelos_chain(model, r1, r2, count=4).

For inner semicircle radii $r_1, r_2 > 0$ and outer radius $R = r_1 + r_2$ along baseline $AC$ ($A = (0, 0)$, $D = (2r_1, 0)$, $C = (2R, 0)$), circle inversion centered at $A$ with squared radius $k^2 = 4 R r_1$ maps $C_{\mathrm{outer}}$ and $C_{\mathrm{left}}$ to the parallel vertical lines $u = 2r_1$ and $u = 2R$, yielding 100% rational coordinates for every circle $C_n$ ($n \ge 1$) with zero square roots:

\[\Delta_n = r_1(r_1 + r_2) + n^2 r_2^2, \qquad r_n = \frac{r_1 r_2 (r_1 + r_2)}{\Delta_n},\]
\[x_n = \frac{r_1 (r_1 + r_2)(2r_1 + r_2)}{\Delta_n}, \qquad y_n = \frac{2 n \, r_1 r_2 (r_1 + r_2)}{\Delta_n} = 2 n r_n = n d_n.\]
Parameters:

  • model_or_r1 – Either a Model or left inner radius $r_1$.

  • r1_or_r2 – Left radius $r_1$ (when model_or_r1 is a Model) or right radius $r_2$.

  • r2_or_count – Right radius $r_2$ (when model_or_r1 is a Model) or chain length count.

  • count – Number of chain circles $n = 1, \dots, \text{count}$ when model_or_r1 is a Model.

  • model – Optional explicit Model keyword argument.

Returns:

Populated ArbelosChainResult.

geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos.verify_arbelos_chain_symbolic(n_max: int = 5) → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Prove Pappus’s Arbelos Theorem symbolically over $\mathbb{Q}(r_1, r_2, n)$.

Verifies four universal polynomial identities in $\mathbb{Q}(r_1, r_2, n)$:

  1. Pappus’s Altitude Law: $y_n - n (2 r_n) = 0$ for symbolic $n$.

  2. Internal Tangency to Outer Semicircle: $Q(K_n, O_0) - (R - r_n)^2 = 0$.

  3. External Tangency to Left Semicircle: $Q(K_n, O_1) - (r_1 + r_n)^2 = 0$.

  4. Chain-to-Chain External Tangency: $Q(K_{n+1}, K_n) - (r_{n+1} + r_n)^2 = 0$ for symbolic $n$ (including $n = 0$, tangency of $C_1$ to $C_{\mathrm{right}}$).

Parameters:

n_max – Optional positive check bound for explicit integer spot-checks alongside symbolic $n$.

Returns:

Dictionary mapping each invariant name to its simplified residual (all sp.Integer(0)).

geometor.pappus.synagoge_demos.verify_honeycomb_isoperimetry(perimeter: ScalarInput = 12, *, build_model: bool = False) → HoneycombIsoperimetryResult¶

Verify Pappus’s Book V Honeycomb Isoperimetry & Regular Planar Tiling Theorem.

Proves two pillars of Synagoge Book V (Preface & Prop. 1, Hultsch I, pp. 304–310):

  1. Uniqueness of Regular Planar Tilings: For $k \ge 3$ congruent regular $n$-gons ($n \ge 3$) to surround a point without gaps or overlaps, their interior angle $\frac{n - 2}{n} \cdot 180^\circ$ must satisfy $k \cdot \frac{n - 2}{n} = 2$, i.e. $k = \frac{2n}{n - 2} = 2 + \frac{4}{n - 2} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 3}$, whose unique integer solutions are $(n - 2) \in \{1, 2, 4\} \iff n \in \{3, 4, 6\}$ with $k \in \{6, 4, 3\}$.

  2. Isoperimetric Superiority of the Hexagon: For common perimeter $P > 0$:

    \[\operatorname{Area}_3 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{36} P^2, \qquad \operatorname{Area}_4 = \frac{1}{16} P^2, \qquad \operatorname{Area}_6 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{24} P^2,\]

    giving $\frac{\operatorname{Area}_6}{\operatorname{Area}_3} = \frac{3}{2}$ and $\frac{\operatorname{Area}_6}{\operatorname{Area}_4} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3} > 1$ (since $\left(\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{3} > 1$).

Parameters:

  • perimeter – Common positive perimeter $P$ (default 12, giving integer sides $4, 3, 2$).

  • build_model – If True, also construct the geometric Model.

Returns:

Populated HoneycombIsoperimetryResult.

Parameters:

output_dir – Destination directory for .svg files.

Returns:

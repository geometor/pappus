geometor.pappus.sphinx_ext¶

Sphinx extension for Pappus of Alexandria’s Synagoge Multi-Track Book Codex.

Registers the el (“Greek (Hultsch 1876)”) companion track in photon_platform.publish.book at build time and configures offline-safe intersphinx_mapping entries against locally built sibling GEOMETOR repositories whenever their docs/objects.inv inventories exist on disk.

geometor.pappus.projective¶

Exact symbolic projective invariants, cross-ratios, and harmonic/golden range scanners.

Provides 100% exact SymPy implementations of collinear cross-ratios, central perspectivities (Book VII, Prop. 129 of Pappus’s Collection), Wildberger quadrances and spreads, collinear harmonic range detection $(A, B; C, D) = -1$, and integration with geometor.divine for golden section discovery.

geometor.pappus.projective.cross_ratio(pt_a: sympy.geometry.Point, pt_b: sympy.geometry.Point, pt_c: sympy.geometry.Point, pt_d: sympy.geometry.Point, *, check_collinear: bool = True) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute the exact projective cross-ratio $(A, B; C, D)$ of four collinear points.

Uses the standard projective convention

\[(A, B; C, D) = \frac{(u_A - u_C)(u_B - u_D)}{(u_A - u_D)(u_B - u_C)},\]

where $u$ is any affine coordinate along the line carrying $A, B, C, D$. Under this convention, $(A, B; C, D) = -1$ iff $(C, D)$ harmonically separates $(A, B)$, and $(0, 1; \phi, \phi + 1) = \phi$.

Parameters:

  • pt_a – First point $A$.

  • pt_b – Second point $B$.

  • pt_c – Third point $C$.

  • pt_d – Fourth point $D$.

  • check_collinear – If True, verify that $A, B, C, D$ are collinear.

Returns:

Exact simplified SymPy expression for $(A, B; C, D)$.

Raises:

ValueError – If the points are not collinear or if the denominator vanishes.

Parameters:

geometor.pappus.projective.find_harmonic_ranges(model: geometor.model.Model, line: sympy.geometry.Line | None = None, *, include_guides: bool = False) → list[HarmonicRange]¶

Scan lines in a Model for exact collinear harmonic tetrads.

Revives and modernizes the analyze_harmonics routine from GEOMETOR’s original 2023 pappus.py exploration. For every candidate line carrying at least four points, points are sorted along the line ($P_1 < P_2 < P_3 < P_4$) so that each unordered harmonic tetrad is reported canonically once via the unique alternating separation $(P_1, P_3; P_2, P_4) = -1$.

Parameters:

  • model – Geometric model to scan.

  • line – Optional single line to inspect; if None, scans all lines in model.

  • include_guides – If True, also include points and lines marked guide=True.

Returns:

List of discovered HarmonicRange records.

geometor.pappus.projective.circle_stereographic_point(t: sympy.Expr | int, *, center: sympy.geometry.Point | tuple[sympy.Expr | int, sympy.Expr | int] = (0, 0), radius: sympy.Expr | int = 1) → sympy.geometry.Point¶

Compute an exact point on $(x - x_0)^2 + (y - y_0)^2 = R^2$ via rational stereographic slope $t$.

Uses the classical Weierstrass / stereographic parameterization from $(-R + x_0, y_0)$:

\[P(t) = \left(x_0 + R \frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2},\; y_0 + R \frac{2 t}{1 + t^2}\right).\]

Every $t \in \mathbb{Q}$ yields an exact rational point in $\mathbb{Q}^2$, and every $t \in \mathbb{Q}(\phi)$ yields an exact point in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})^2$.

Parameters:

  • t – Exact symbolic or integer stereographic parameter.

  • center – Circle center $(x_0, y_0)$.

  • radius – Exact positive circle radius $R$.

Returns:

Exact Point on the circle.

geometor.pappus.projective.conic_equation_through_points(points: Sequence[sympy.geometry.Point | tuple[sympy.Expr | int, sympy.Expr | int]]) → sympy.Expr¶

Construct the exact quadratic conic polynomial $C(x, y)$ through five points.

Evaluates the $6 \times 6$ Veronese determinant

\[\begin{split}\det \begin{bmatrix} x^2 & x y & y^2 & x & y & 1 \\ x_0^2 & x_0 y_0 & y_0^2 & x_0 & y_0 & 1 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ x_4^2 & x_4 y_4 & y_4^2 & x_4 & y_4 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

in the symbols x, y.

Parameters:

points – Sequence of 5 points in the plane.

Returns:

Expanded SymPy polynomial in x and y (zero iff the 5 points are degenerate with 4+ collinear in a way that fails to determine a unique conic).

Raises:

ValueError – If len(points) != 5.

geometor.pappus.projective.are_coconic(points: Sequence[sympy.geometry.Point | tuple[sympy.Expr | int, sympy.Expr | int]]) → bool¶

Return True iff all given points lie on a single conic (smooth or degenerate line-pair).

Unifies Pappus’s degenerate two-line configuration ($L_a \cdot L_b = 0$) and Pascal’s smooth conic configuration ($A x^2 + B x y + C y^2 + D x + E y + F = 0$) via the rank of the $N \times 6$ Veronese matrix $[x_k^2, x_k y_k, y_k^2, x_k, y_k, 1]$.

Parameters:

points – Sequence of planar points (any $\le 5$ points trivially lie on at least one conic).

Returns:

True if the Veronese matrix has rank $\le 5$, else False.

geometor.pappus.projective.conic_cross_ratio(p1: sympy.geometry.Point, p2: sympy.geometry.Point, p3: sympy.geometry.Point, p4: sympy.geometry.Point, observer: sympy.geometry.Point) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute Michel Chasles’s projective cross-ratio $(P_1, P_2; P_3, P_4)_{\mathcal{C}}$ on a conic.

By Chasles’s Theorem, for four distinct points $P_1, P_2, P_3, P_4$ on an irreducible conic $\mathcal{C}$, the cross-ratio of the pencil of four rays $[V P_1], [V P_2], [V P_3], [V P_4]$ cut by any transversal line is independent of the fifth point $V = \text{observer} \in \mathcal{C}$.

Parameters:

  • p1 – First conic point.

  • p2 – Second conic point.

  • p3 – Third conic point.

  • p4 – Fourth conic point.

  • observer – Distinct fifth point $V$ on the same conic.

Returns:

Exact simplified SymPy cross-ratio expression (or sp.oo).

Raises:

ValueError – If the 5 points are not distinct or not coconic.

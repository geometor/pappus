geometor.pappus.pascal¶

Symbolic DAG constructions and combinatorics for Blaise Pascal’s Hexagrammum Mysticum (1640).

Generalizes Pappus’s Hexagon Theorem from a degenerate line-pair $a \cup b$ to six points inscribed on a smooth conic $\mathcal{C}$ (circle or ellipse):

  • Pascal’s Mystic Hexagram (Essai pour les coniques, Feb. 1640): for any cyclic 6-tuple $\langle P_{c_0}, P_{c_1}, P_{c_2}, P_{c_3}, P_{c_4}, P_{c_5} \rangle$ on a conic, opposite side intersections $M_{03}, M_{14}, M_{25}$ lie on a single Pascal line.

  • Bi-Triangle :math:`S_3` Steiner Family: partitioning the 6 conic vertices into two complementary triangles $\triangle_A$ and $\triangle_B$ generates 6 alternating hexagons whose 3 even (\(A_3\)) Pascal lines concur at \(G_+\) and whose 3 odd (\(S_3 \setminus A_3\)) Pascal lines concur at \(G_-\)—directly generalizing Pappus’s two Star points \((S_+, S_-)\)!

  • Complete 60-Line *Hexagrammum Mysticum* Catalog: exact projective enumeration of all 15 chords, 45 diagonal Pascal points, 60 Pascal lines, 20 Jakob Steiner points, 60 Thomas Kirkman points, and 15 Julius Plücker lines.

  • Universal Symbolic Proof & Golden Conic: exact 6-parameter polynomial proof over $\mathbb{Z}[t_0, t_1, t_2, t_3, t_4, t_5]$ and Golden Ratio conic perspectivities over $\mathbb{Q}(\phi)$.

geometor.pappus.pascal.canonical_hexagon_cycle(cycle: Sequence[int]) → Cycle6¶

Normalize a 6-vertex permutation up to dihedral rotation and reversal ($D_6$).

Rotates cycle so 0 appears first and picks the lexicographically smaller orientation between (0, c1, c2, c3, c4, c5) and (0, c5, c4, c3, c2, c1).

Parameters:

cycle – Permutation of (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).

Returns:

Canonical 6-tuple representative among the $6! / 12 = 60$ distinct hexagons.

Raises:

ValueError – If cycle is not a permutation of 0..5.

geometor.pappus.pascal.construct_pascal_steiner_family(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None, points: Sequence[PointInput] | None = None, *, preset: str = 'hexagrammum', triangle_a: tuple[int, int, int] = (0, 2, 4), triangle_b: tuple[int, int, int] = (1, 3, 5), include_conic: bool = True, scan_harmonics: bool = True, enable_divine: bool = False) → PascalSteinerFamilyResult¶

Construct the 6-hexagon \(S_3\) bi-triangle Pascal family and its two Steiner points.

Partitions the 6 conic vertices into $\triangle_A = (P_{a_0}, P_{a_1}, P_{a_2})$ and $\triangle_B = (P_{b_0}, P_{b_1}, P_{b_2})$. Permuting $\triangle_B$ by $\sigma \in S_3$ forms 6 alternating hexagons $\langle A_0, B_{\sigma_0}, A_1, B_{\sigma_1}, A_2, B_{\sigma_2} \rangle$ whose 3 even (\(A_3\)) Pascal lines concur at \(G_+\) (ID "S1") and whose 3 odd (\(S_3 \setminus A_3\)) Pascal lines concur at \(G_-\) (ID "S2").

Parameters:

  • model – Optional existing Model.

  • points – Optional sequence of 6 coconic points; defaults to preset.

  • preset – Key in PASCAL_PRESETS used when points is None.

  • triangle_a – Indices $(a_0, a_1, a_2)$ of the first inscribed triangle.

  • triangle_b – Complementary indices $(b_0, b_1, b_2)$ of the second triangle.

  • include_conic – If True, register the carrier circle or ellipse.

  • scan_harmonics – If True, scan the configuration for collinear harmonic tetrads.

  • enable_divine – If True, attach geometor.divine and scan golden sections.

Returns:

Populated PascalSteinerFamilyResult.

Raises:

ValueError – If triangle_a and triangle_b do not partition 0..5 or points are not coconic.

geometor.pappus.pascal.enumerate_hexagrammum_mysticum(points: Sequence[PointInput] | None = None, *, preset: str = 'hexagrammum') → HexagrammumMysticumCatalog¶

Enumerate and verify the complete 60-line Hexagrammum Mysticum web in $\mathbb{P}^2$.

Computes in exact homogeneous coordinates:

  1. 15 Chords $[P_i P_j]$ ($0 \le i < j \le 5$).

  2. 45 Diagonal Pascal Points $[P_i P_j] \cap [P_k P_l]$ on disjoint vertex pairs.

  3. 60 Pascal Lines across all $6! / 12 = 60$ cyclic hexagons, verifying 3-point collinearity.

  4. 20 Jakob Steiner Points ($10$ triangle partitions $\times 2$ \(S_3\) parities), verifying 3-line concurrency at each point.

  5. 60 Thomas Kirkman Points, verifying 3-line concurrency on each syntheme Kirkman triple.

  6. 15 Julius Plücker Lines, each carrying 4 of the 20 Steiner points.

Parameters:

  • points – Optional sequence of 6 coconic points; defaults to preset.

  • preset – Key in PASCAL_PRESETS used when points is None.

Returns:

Populated HexagrammumMysticumCatalog.

Raises:

ValueError – If fewer/more than 6 distinct coconic points are given.

geometor.pappus.pascal.verify_pascal_symbolic() → sympy.Expr¶

Prove Pascal’s Hexagrammum Mysticum universally over $\mathbb{Z}[t_0, t_1, t_2, t_3, t_4, t_5]$.

Parameterizes six generic points on the projective conic $X^2 + Y^2 = Z^2$ via $P_k = [1 - t_k^2 : 2 t_k : 1 + t_k^2]$, computes the three opposite-side meets $M_{03}, M_{14}, M_{25}$ via 3D vector cross products, and expands the $3 \times 3$ collinearity determinant $\det[M_{03}, M_{14}, M_{25}]$, returning 0 identically.

Returns:

Simplified SymPy polynomial expression (identically sp.Integer(0)).
